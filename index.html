Make smiles. Drive near festival-goers and send bubbles their way. Chain smiles for a bigger score, and avoid crashing into people and props.

Discover bubbles. Explore stages, meet Lurleen and stay out after dark to unlock special bubbles. Open Bubble Upgrades from the main menu or with the in-game heart (U on keyboard) to see clues and switch earned bubbles on or off. Every discovery adds bubble charm.

Keep Zerble bubbling. Grab glowing juice jugs or visit a bubble vendor. In Festival Run, each new day brings scarcer juice and pricier refills. Hitting people or drawing frowns while dry raises the vibe meter. A marshal whistles at the warning mark; fill the meter and they walk you out. Staying empty too long also ends the run. Just Cruisin' keeps free refills and no run-ending pressure.
