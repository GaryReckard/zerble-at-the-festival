Make smiles. Drive near festival-goers and send bubbles their way. Chain smiles for a bigger score, and avoid crashing into people and props.

Give lifts. Some festival-goers wave with a thought bubble showing where they want to go. Pull up beside one and stop, and they will hop on. The Passenger rides panel names their destination. Hammocks and porta-potties are flexible: any matching spot works. Other destinations are specific. Follow the arrow for a suggestion, then slow down beside a suitable stop to let them off for a burst of smiles.

Discover bubbles. Explore stages, meet Lurleen and stay out after dark to unlock special bubbles. Open Bubble Upgrades from the main menu or with the in-game heart (U on keyboard) to see clues and switch earned bubbles on or off. Every discovery adds bubble charm.

Keep Zerble bubbling. Grab glowing juice jugs or visit a bubble vendor. In Festival Run, each new day brings scarcer juice and pricier refills. Hitting people or drawing frowns while dry raises the vibe meter. A marshal whistles at the warning mark; fill the meter and they walk you out. Staying empty too long also ends the run. Just Cruisin' keeps free refills and no marshal pressure. In either mode, hard crashes damage your cart and deep water can end your trip.
