ZERBLE MENU REDESIGN HANDOFF | Prepared November 13, 2026

HOW TO USE
Upload this ZIP to Claude Design, unzip if the interface asks, and paste the contents of 01-PROMPT.txt into the conversation. Select your intended model in Claude Design. No model availability or attachment support is assumed by this packet.

If ZIP ingestion is unavailable, upload 01-PROMPT.txt, 03-FUNCTIONALITY.txt, 02-SCREEN-GUIDE.txt, CONTACT-SHEET.jpg, and the relevant full-size JPGs. Every individual file and the ZIP are below 20,000,000 bytes. The original artwork is optional reference material.

CONTENTS
01-PROMPT.txt: Ready-to-paste design assignment.
02-SCREEN-GUIDE.txt: Screenshot inventory, contexts, and limitations.
03-FUNCTIONALITY.txt: Source-verified functions, flows, and required states.
04-PROVENANCE.txt: Capture method and source locations.
CONTACT-SHEET.jpg: Overview of all captures.
GALLERY.html: Offline screenshot browser, with captions and full-size image links.
screenshots/: Full-size JPEG captures, numbered to match the guide.
reference-art/: Existing title artwork layers, logo, bubble, and menu planks.

SCOPE
The ordinary player-facing title and menus, plus results, map, and in-game launchers. Developer/debug tools and hidden effect-tuning panels are intentionally excluded from the public UI assignment. There is no request to redesign gameplay, replace the game engine, or implement these designs in the repository.

The design should preserve functionality while remaining free to change visual language, grouping, and navigation. Current screenshots are evidence of today’s interface, not a requirement to keep its weaknesses.
