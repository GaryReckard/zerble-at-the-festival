## TL;DR

We investigated larger changes to how the game calculates ground contact, tracks objects and presents repeated characters. The tested mechanisms did not show enough net benefit to justify a rewrite, and the second council independently agreed to stop with no selection. The conditional exhaustive production plan was therefore not activated.

## The Problem

Gary reported poor performance on an older iPad and asked whether larger restructuring could produce gains beyond small local fixes. The investigation seeks useful reductions while preserving population, terrain, physics, simulation behavior and freedom to move or switch cameras. Desktop experiments cannot establish the iPad outcome.

## Proposed Fix

The approach is to inventory the actual mechanisms, isolate promising replacements in disposable experiments, and advance only a candidate whose net benefit justifies its complexity. The outcome is an explicit decision to stop with no selected rewrite. No production proposal, delta capability specification, design or apply-ready migration was created because none earned selection; the checklist records the completed investigation rather than runtime implementation.

## Key Decisions

The qualified November 6 terrain source remains the baseline. Support certificates passed exercised exactness but showed no stable net warm benefit; explicit registry publication saved only about 0.13 ms/frame in a controlled Node fixture and regressed with all movers. The rendering probe lowered draws without a meaningful render-work reduction. See [the evidence bundle](../../../verification/performance/2026-11-07-structural-options/README.md) and [decisions](session-log.md).

## Risks & Watch-outs

Sampled attribution is not removable elapsed time, fewer drawing requests are not necessarily faster, and a low cumulative construction total does not rule out rare stalls. Frozen rendering and Node fixtures cannot establish live driving, long-run retention, Auto quality or mobile Safari performance. Untested mechanisms remain possible; a no-selection verdict must not claim every possible rewrite has been disproved.
