## Architect's Position

**TL/DR:** Close this investigation with no selected production rewrite. The completed mechanisms did not earn advancement, and the residual candidates require attribution before their architectural cost is justified. That is a finite selection result for this inventory, not a conclusion that further structural opportunities do not exist. The prior council explicitly permits this outcome and conditions the remaining mechanisms on specific evidence (`deliberations/001-options/results.md:57-59,97`).

Evidence filenames below refer to `verification/performance/2026-11-07-structural-options/`. The reviewed manifest identifies application `00d10db620720b429a5ab93e6973495324b8d2e0c33e7b98ea6d22dedf64b708` (`source-manifest.json:app`). This position used read-only source/evidence inspection and wrote only this markdown file.

### Priority Sequence

1. **Reject the tested implementations for production selection without generalizing their failures.** Exact support reuse passed its exercised oracle cases but worsened construction/first use and showed order-dependent warm results. Registry ownership passed 16,948 comparisons but saved approximately 0.134 ms/frame only under an assumed three-mover classification and regressed under all-entry publication. Neither establishes a worthwhile live-game migration (`support-probe-report.md:19-23,45-59`; `registry-probe-report.md:3-17,39-65`).
2. **Honor the rendering discriminator's declared exit.** The summary records zero qualifying views for both mechanisms in both tiers across 4,608 frozen renders. Booth flags removed local composition while retaining world multiplications, and shopkeeper batching retained source rigs. The result closes those mechanisms, rather than compact poses, adult culling or all hierarchy elimination (`render-summary.json:tiers.*.frames,tiers.*.nomination,tiers.*.rows[*].matrixAudit`; `render-probe-report.md:50-83`).
3. **Complete selection by reviewing residual mechanisms, then stop without a production plan.** The source facts below do not provide an attributable removable workload large enough to select another redesign. Further diagnostics are possible, but are not mandatory merely because a mechanism remains plausible. This follows the existing advancement rule rather than silently replacing it with a requirement to prototype every inventory row (`deliberations/001-options/results.md:57-59,97`; `render-discriminator-policy.md:77-81`).

### Structural Risks Identified

- **Adult presentation contains support authority.** `_writeMatrices` calls `groundNpc` before publishing transforms, so gating the function on camera visibility would also gate grounding. Adult meshes deliberately disable frustum culling to prevent moving actors disappearing under stale bounds, and draw counts follow the highest occupied slot. A future presentation boundary must leave grounding, actor identity and accepted-frame behavior authoritative in Crowd, while publishing independently bounded draw slots and all matching appearance attributes. Simply enabling culling or skipping the writer is not that boundary (`src/crowd.js:511-522,925-948,1628-1645`; `ARCHITECTURE.md:973-989`).
- **The strongest remaining presentation lead is still unmeasured.** Direct inspection of the three profile JSON files confirms adult `_writeMatrices` inclusive attribution of 1,525.613 / 924.192 / 770.441 ms for High route / Low route / High parked, compared with self attribution of 509.281 / 320.075 / 251.614 ms. These are sampled cumulative rows, not removable elapsed rendering cost, and the source grounding call explains why inclusive attribution cannot be assigned wholesale to presentation. No eligible camera/shadow fraction or upload bottleneck has been established (`high-route-all-functions.json:self,inclusive`; `low-route-all-functions.json:self,inclusive`; `high-parked-all-functions.json:self,inclusive`; `src/crowd.js:1628-1635`; `residual-cost-triage.md:9,19`).
- **Compact child poses are distinct from draw batching.** The child presenter preserves the original hierarchy, disables original mesh layers, copies matrices into instances and maintains bounds. Removing those rigs would require a different pose representation and exact transformed-parent, visibility, layer and caster behavior. The High-route scene-wide matrix self total of 3,315.538 ms does not identify the children's share; the identified presenter inclusive total is 232.272 ms. Neither number selects compact poses without subtree attribution (`src/people/childPresentation.js:23-25,34-54,69-115`; `high-route-all-functions.json:self,inclusive`).
- **The child solver's correction order is behavioral state.** Its inline loop corrects pairs sequentially, then applies world constraints before the next pass, and exits early on settlement. A stale once-per-pass neighbor roster can miss pairs created by earlier corrections. Final grounding, animation and collider publication follow the solver, so the entire update's sampled total is not a solver timer. Require observed passes, pair corrections and exact section timing before selecting an extraction or spatial replacement (`src/people/children.js:415-466`; `high-route-all-functions.json:inclusive`; `residual-cost-triage.md:20`).
- **Registry feasibility does not prove production mutation coverage.** The current scan discovers live geometry/class changes, refreshes maximum query radii and reconstructs ordered oversized entries at the vehicle-before-adult barrier. An explicit-publication redesign must preserve that barrier, same-pass add/remove visibility and ordered queries, including later writers whose grid changes currently wait until the next barrier. The prototype's certified-static negative fixture already demonstrates the failure when ownership is asserted incorrectly (`src/registry.js:305-350`; `src/main.js:1463-1472`; `registry-source-map.md:22-39`; `registry-probe-report.md:13-17`).
- **Staging cannot be introduced as incidental yielding.** Chunk deadlines are checked before whole generation, while tree entries publish during construction. Existing district staging transfers registry references only at completion, which is the appropriate ownership precedent if exact-frame builder evidence later justifies this candidate. Small cumulative chunk attribution does not eliminate rare stalls, but neither does it identify their cause. Ground recentering is likewise intermittent and needs exact-frame attribution before a tile/ring representation is selected (`src/chunks.js:385-397,1081-1088`; `src/landscape/district.js:145-157`; `src/world.js:182-190,211-228`; `streaming-source-map.md:104-129`; `residual-cost-triage.md:22-23`).
- **Presentation must borrow resources without acquiring world ownership.** Future batching must detach each owner's contributions and dispose only its own buffers; chunk, forest and lake lifetimes remain independent, and lake colliders must retain their deliberate lack of `chunkKey`. Shared-resource disposal guards and instance dirty flags remain mandatory. The current presenter illustrates restoring source layers and removing its observer while disposing batch objects rather than borrowed geometries/materials (`AGENTS.md:99-107`; `ARCHITECTURE.md:196-222`; `src/people/childPresentation.js:104-115`).
- **Historical templates must not reintroduce obsolete module plumbing.** Current Vite imports discover modules, while Three remains pinned and routed through `threeShim.js`. No importmap inventories, framework or generalized entity infrastructure are justified by these negative experiments (`AGENTS.md:86-93`; `.claude/rules/no-build.md:14-28`).

### Finite Advancement and Stopping Rule

**Stop now:** preserve the negative probes, the source manifest, and a precise statement that none of the inventoried candidates earned production planning from this investigation. Do not create an exhaustive migration plan for an unselected mechanism. The rendering filter failed, the support prototype had no stable net timing advantage, and registry ownership lacks both production certification and material route benefit (`render-summary.json:decision`; `support-probe-report.md:45-59`; `registry-probe-report.md:59-65`). This stopping rule accords with the project's distinction between correctness completeness and bounded performance investment (`.claude/rules/performance.md:223-229`).

**Conditional future experiment, not an accepted recommendation:** if the investigation is explicitly extended, the most informative single discriminator is an adult camera/caster/upload census separated from support and animation. It could materially change selection by revealing either a large avoidable offscreen submission workload or too little eligible work to offset spatial-batch migration, bounds and draw costs. Include arbitrary camera movement, High shadows, Low, source slot occupancy and matching per-instance attributes. Do not implement a spatial presenter merely to collect the census (`src/crowd.js:511-522,925-948,1628-1645`; `residual-cost-triage.md:19,31`).

The census would nominate a bounded equal-content prototype only if its measured opportunity could plausibly clear a newly declared investment threshold. It would not itself nominate production. Any later finalist still needs exact pose/publication equivalence, streaming teardown and maintenance accounting, protected camera/shadow/triangle behavior, ordinary calibrated parked/driving comparisons and separate device acceptance. Frozen render work and sampled inclusive attribution cannot substitute for those gates (`deliberations/001-options/results.md:61-85`; `render-probe-report.md:103-109`).

### Verdict

- **Verdict**: Proceed with mitigations.
- **Key Concern**: A plausible presentation rewrite still mixes support authority, draw publication and lifecycle obligations without sufficient mechanism-specific benefit evidence.
- **Recommendation**: Proceed to a documented no-selected-rewrite conclusion and close the present investigation. Retain the adult census as a conditional future discriminator, without requiring it or treating it as an accepted production direction.
