### Alternative Approach

If later evidence reopens adult presentation, test **packing eligible actors into the existing global batches before introducing spatial tiles**. Keep stable simulation identities and complete updates, but publish a dense render-slot mapping that omits actors irrelevant to the active view and required shadow submissions. This is a proposed alternative, not implemented behavior or an accepted experiment. It challenges the assumption that spatial ownership is necessary to reduce submitted adult work: current global batches already exist, whereas highest-slot drawing is the present policy (`src/crowd.js:438-470,939-956`).

- **Value**: This hypothesis targets instance eligibility while retaining the present batch count, which could avoid the extra draw and lifecycle surface of spatial cells. It preserves festival density and offscreen interactions by changing presentation only. Both benefits remain conditional on the actual visibility/caster census and timed publication cost (`src/crowd.js:511-522,939-956`; `ARCHITECTURE.md:973-989`).
- **Risk**: Dense packing must move colors, skin, shirt attributes, articulated pose/depth data and accessory mappings consistently; it must preserve offscreen casters and account for camera changes before rendering. Repacking could cost more than the avoided GPU work. Camera-only removal on shadow tiers would be invalid (`src/crowd.js:438-509`; `src/people/childPresentation.js:85-106`).
- **Effort**: Potentially smaller than spatial tiling because it proposes no new world-cell ownership, but it is still a substantial presentation change with an unproved benefit. It cannot skip current grounding or turn rendering into simulation authority (`src/crowd.js:1628-1632`; `ARCHITECTURE.md:973-989`).

### Finite Stopping and Reopening Rule

**Stop now:** No candidate is selected for exhaustive production planning. Preserve the negative results, the baseline identity and the residual hypotheses, with their missing evidence. This closes the requested finite selection exercise without describing untested mechanisms as disproved (`briefing.md:5-16`; `../001-options/results.md:42-44,57-59`).

**Reopen only on new mechanism-specific evidence:** For adults, require frame-linked actor/view/caster eligibility and matrix-upload accounting separated from grounding, followed by a useful net publication/render result. For child solving, require observed passes, checks and solver-section cost. For compact poses, require child-subtree attribution. For construction, require exact-frame builder/recenter attribution. These are conditional entry gates, not four newly authorized workstreams, and they address the specific gaps documented above (`src/crowd.js:923-956,1628-1632`; `src/people/children.js:415-465`; `src/people/childPresentation.js:49-55`; `src/chunks.js:385-397`; `src/world.js:182-190`).

Any future finalist must still pass equal-content ordinary parked/driving timing, lifecycle and visual checks, with device acceptance kept separate. The frozen render filter cannot substitute for those gates, and neither draw counts nor reduced queries constitute a delivered-frame result (`render-discriminator-policy.md:51-72`; `../001-options/results.md:54-59`). Existing phone/Auto acceptance remains explicitly open in `ROADMAP.md:257-267`; this decision should not silently replace it with another desktop rewrite investigation.

### Verdict

- **Verdict**: Proceed with mitigations.
- **Key Concern**: Continuing until some mechanism wins would turn a finite search for player value into an expanding obligation to redesign plausible systems.
- **Recommendation**: Proceed to the evidence handoff with **no selected rewrite**, require no further runtime discriminator for this decision, and create no exhaustive implementation plan. Retain adult presentation as a source-grounded reopening hypothesis with the narrower packing alternative above, while preserving every ordinary-frame and device gate.
