## Profiler's Position

**TL/DR:** Stop this investigation with no selected production rewrite. The tested mechanisms did not earn advancement, and the remaining inventory lacks mechanism-specific removable-cost evidence. Adult presentation remains a concrete future lead, but it does not presently justify exhaustive planning or require another runtime experiment to support this scoped stopping decision.

The rendering policy explicitly requires residual review rather than manufacturing a winner (`openspec/changes/structural-performance-redesign/deliberations/001-options/results.md`, Change Groups 2–3; `verification/performance/2026-11-07-structural-options/render-discriminator-policy.md`, Decision rule).

### Budget Estimate

- **Draw delta:** +0 for the recommendation to stop. The frozen baseline rows span 760–1,287 Low draws and 776–1,994 High draws, already above the nominal 80/200/400 Low/Mid/High guides. Those are diagnostic view ranges, not ordinary device acceptance. Low has the tightest nominal allowance; no Mid capture establishes its actual margin (`verification/performance/2026-11-07-structural-options/render-summary.json`, `tiers.*.rows[*].submissions.baselineMaxCalls`; `src/debug.js:35-46`).
- **Triangle delta:** +0 for stopping. Frozen baseline rows span 423,914–521,274 Low triangles and 780,641–1,068,709 High triangles against the nominal 150k/400k/1.2M guides. Tested batching leaves Low triangles equal and adds up to 1,056 High triangles, so saved draws do not imply proportionally saved geometry (`render-summary.json`, same submission rows; `render-probe-report.md:76-83`; both in the evidence directory above).

Counters exceeding nominal guides do not identify which rewrite wins, and the project explicitly says those guides need recalibration (`.claude/rules/performance.md`, 2026-10-08 audit and Audit order). ### Independent Residual Assessment

I checked the three complete profile exports directly, rather than treating the residual triage's ranking as a conclusion. Their scope explicitly distinguishes sampling attribution from elapsed timers. Adult `_writeMatrices` inclusive attribution is 1,525.613 / 924.192 / 770.441 ms for High route / Low route / High parked, but grounding is inside that function, so culling cannot claim that entire total. Its self attribution is 509.281 / 320.075 / 251.614 ms, which also is not a saving forecast (`verification/performance/2026-11-07-structural-options/{high-route,low-route,high-parked}-all-functions.json`, `self` and `inclusive` rows; `src/crowd.js:1628-1645`).

Adult presentation is the strongest remaining representation lead because global batches explicitly disable culling and draws extend through the highest allocated slot. Spatial publication could remove offscreen vertex/upload work, but could also add batch draws, compaction, bounds and shadow maintenance. The missing visibility/caster/upload census could change that tradeoff; neither the measured source-writer cost nor shopkeeper batching supplies it (`src/crowd.js:511-522,923-952`; `render-actor-source-map.md`, Candidate 3). That makes another experiment defensible as a new conditional investigation, not necessary to complete this selection.

Child update attribution is 2,169.750 / 1,686.217 / 988.955 ms, but it combines behavior, support, the inline pair solver and final pose/collider publication. Its 128-pass ceiling is not an observed pass count, and corrections move positions during the ordered pair walk (`*-all-functions.json`, inclusive child `update`; `src/people/children.js:415-465`). A spatial solver needs frame-linked pass/correction timing before planning, not an algorithm chosen from the worst-case bound.

Compact child poses target retained rig traversal, distinct from draw batching. Presenter `sync` inclusive attribution is only 232.272 / 215.765 / 182.801 ms, while scene-wide matrix self attribution is 3,315.538 / 2,252.132 / 1,514.832 ms. The latter cannot be assigned to children without subtree attribution (`*-all-functions.json`, named rows; `src/people/childPresentation.js:23-25,69-106`). Ordinary generation and ground recentering likewise remain possible tail contributors, but require exact frame linkage; recentering is conditional and recomputes geometry, normals and bounds (`src/world.js:182-191,211-228`; `streaming-source-map.md`, Main finding and measurement limit).

### Selection and Finite Stopping Rule

The stopping rule is met when completed prototypes fail their declared advancement gates and the remaining inventoried candidates have no isolated evidence of materially removable work that warrants production planning. This is a finite engineering selection, not proof that every future structural optimization is ineffective. Here support lacks stable net benefit, registry's absolute controlled benefit is small with ownership unproved, rendering misses its declared filter, and residual attribution does not establish another finalist (`support-probe-report.md:45-59`; `registry-probe-report.md:39-65`; `render-probe-report.md:41-83`; the residual source/profile checks above).

If the investigation is independently extended, choose only an adult presentation census first: record live versus submitted slots, current camera-intersecting actor bounds, shadow-relevant bounds and upload bytes, separating support from presentation. A negligible removable population or CPU/GPU workload ends that branch; a substantial opportunity nominates one isolated spatial-publication experiment, whose acceptance policy must be declared before timing. Counts alone never accept the rewrite. Any later prototype must include its own maintenance, allocation/unload costs and all-camera shadow/appearance correctness, followed by ordinary calibrated parked/driving comparisons and physical-device acceptance. This conditional path follows the protected-contract and delivered-frame gates in the first council's Change Groups 1–3; it is not an accepted production recommendation.

### Verdict

- **Verdict:** Proceed with mitigations.
- **Key Concern:** Over-budget counters and broad sampled attribution can motivate diagnosis but cannot establish removable elapsed work or select a major rewrite.
- **Recommendation:** Complete the investigation with no selected rewrite and no exhaustive production plan. Record the failed mechanisms and bounded residual unknowns, while retaining adult presentation as a specific future diagnostic lead rather than an implementation commitment.
