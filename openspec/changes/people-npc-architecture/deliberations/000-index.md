# People and NPC architecture deliberation

## Deliberation Index

- [001-architecture synthesis](001-architecture/results.md): Tier 3 synthesis with
  Architect, Auditor, Pragmatist, Profiler, Adversary, and Mediator. All five specialist
  positions completed and recommended proceeding with mitigations. No debate round ran.
- [Independent Astra review](../astra-review.md): requested separately by Gary and
  incorporated by the mediator as supplemental evidence, not a sixth council persona.

The result favors a small registered population runtime with clear identity,
authority, activity, rendering, and cleanup boundaries. It requires a complete
Healing Arts ownership/motion slice, then children, before broad migration and
preserves non-person frame dependencies instead of imposing a flat schedule.

The final [design](../design.md), [specification](../specs/people-runtime/spec.md),
and [tasks](../tasks.md) incorporate the result; [review resolutions](../review-resolution.md)
map the findings to concrete changes. The `001-architecture/input-plan/` tree retains
the reviewed documents with original line numbering, so initial review citations
remain traceable after revision. No runtime implementation or measurement is claimed.
