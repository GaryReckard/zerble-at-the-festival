## Profiler's Position

**TL/DR:** Proceed with the controller-level runtime and stable handles, while making query work, render publication, and measurement conditions explicit acceptance contracts. This is a structural refactor with a zero graphics delta, not a performance optimization. The plan already preserves production batching and avoids a speculative simulation scheduler; those are the correct boundaries.

### Priority Sequence

1. **Characterize costs alongside behavior before creating the runtime.** Keep the proposed Low/Mid baseline task, but record effective adaptive-quality state, physical render dimensions, foreground status, warmed versus first-use frames, and separate timings for people simulation, collision publication, renderer publication, and spawn/recycle. Source: `tasks.md:15-18`, `design.md:217-222`, `src/main.js:225-242`, `ARCHITECTURE.md:581-595`.
2. **Introduce controller dispatch and handles over existing storage.** Validate capability shapes and compile stable phase/provider lists at registration rather than discovering capabilities, sorting dependencies, or rebuilding views every frame. This is a proposed implementation constraint supporting the plan's reusable context and no-wrapper contract, not a claim that such overhead exists today. Source: `design.md:62-83`, `design.md:87-110`, `design.md:126-130`.
3. **Migrate collision and neighborhood interfaces while preserving their current work bounds.** Keep registry-local queries, pooled collision wrappers, existing source order, crowd exclusions, and existing consumer population filters. Add operation counters before replacing loops, then compare candidates visited and callback calls with identical fixtures. Source: `src/main.js:1514-1539`, `src/registry.js:172-178`, `src/oneWheels.js:72-87`, `design.md:170-183`.
4. **Extract the renderer facade without an intermediate population-sized pose copy or repeated flushes.** Keep batch ownership, packed slot ranges, appearance attributes, and dirty writes within the renderer; permit an activity to dirty the affected person's binding after the crowd's earlier simulation slot. Test late release, hidden-rig restoration, and distant slot reuse. Source: `src/crowd.js:399-457`, `src/crowd.js:953-985`, `src/healingArtsActivity.js:40-50`, `src/main.js:1196`, `src/main.js:1242`, `tasks.md:39-48`.
5. **Verify each migration slice on comparable Low/Mid conditions and stop at preservation.** Keep allocation stalls separate from steady-state timing and retain lifetime resource checks through unload/revisit. Do not add an optimization backlog, density rebalance, or scheduling LOD to this migration. Source: `.claude/rules/performance.md:103-116`, `ROADMAP.md:53-56`, `ROADMAP.md:106-120`, `design.md:33-37`, `tasks.md:67-72`.

### Budget Estimate

- **Draw delta**: Target **+0 scene draws and +0 shadow draws** for an identical population, pose, camera, tier, and effective quality state. Current adults use seven base InstancedMeshes plus separate pooled appearance/prop meshes, not one draw per person (`src/crowd.js:399-412`). No new lights or passes are required by the proposed architecture (`design.md:156-162`).
- **Triangle delta**: Target **+0 submitted triangles** under the same conditions, including preserved active instance counts and packed hair/photographer ranges (`src/crowd.js:960-985`). Merely retaining geometry is insufficient if extraction restores maximum pool counts.
- **Tier reference**: Low **80 draws / 150k triangles**, Mid **200 / 400k**, High **400 / 1.2M**, as retained in `openspec/council/charter.md`, Non-Negotiable 6. Low has the smallest nominal envelope, but the closest tier to its limit cannot be identified without fresh comparable captures. These are guide budgets; current policy explicitly calls for recalibration, so HUD color is not the acceptance verdict (`.claude/rules/performance.md:48-51`, `ROADMAP.md:222-224`).
- **Cost type**: **Both allocation stalls and steady-state FPS**. Stable handles add bounded lifetime bookkeeping; the runtime should add controller-level dispatch rather than per-person dispatch layers. No timing improvement is promised without measurement (`design.md:87-102`, `design.md:217-222`).
- **Low/mid-tier verdict**: **Safe in architectural scope, subject to the preservation checks below**. Current Low DPR cap is **1.25**, not the older 1.0 heuristic (`src/perf.js:98-112`, `ARCHITECTURE.md:566-575`). Similarly, the current streaming wall can admit several cheap chunks; do not impose an unrelated one-chunk-per-frame rewrite (`src/perf.js:110-112`, `ARCHITECTURE.md:579-581`). The historic 56-caster audit is context, not a fresh census (`.claude/rules/performance.md:91`).

### Required Plan Amendments

1. **Add a bounded-work acceptance scenario to the people-runtime spec.** After warmup, registration/inspection remain off the ordinary per-person hot path; queries retain caller scratch and current provider filters; collision publication retains pooled wrappers; no new per-person-per-frame handles, poses, or capability lists are allocated. Compare operation counts as well as timings. This strengthens the existing no-query-wrapper requirement without demanding zero allocations in the entire legacy game (`specs/people-runtime/spec.md:28-38`, `src/crowd.js:921-947`, `src/main.js:1514-1538`).
2. **Specify render publication semantics before extracting the facade.** The facade owns slot matrices, packed counts, attributes, shadow policy, culling policy, and dirty flags. A narrow binding update must not force a complete second population traversal. Preserve existing distance gates independently from gameplay ticks. Extend the renderer task with late handoff and slot-count fixtures (`tasks.md:39`, `src/crowd.js:460-470`, `src/crowd.js:953-985`, `src/chunks.js:68-75`).
3. **Make the baseline comparison reproducible and attach it to each affected slice.** Record `renderer.__sceneInfo` draws/triangles, effective quality and dimensions, active population/claims, warmed timing distributions, spawn/recycle outliers, and resource counts after equivalent lifecycle cycles. Discard comparisons across quality transitions; investigate repeated regressions rather than prescribing a universal arbitrary millisecond allowance. The plan's final comparison is necessary, but local comparisons make the responsible migration identifiable (`tasks.md:18`, `tasks.md:69`, `src/main.js:225-242`, `design.md:217-222`).

### Verdict

- **Verdict**: Proceed with mitigations.
- **Key Concern**: A clean common interface can silently multiply query and render work even while preserving behavior and draw counts.
- **Recommendation**: Adopt the controller-level runtime, stable handles, and explicit activity ownership, then incorporate the three acceptance amendments above. Keep the full ECS, population director, scheduling LOD, and universal world-registry identity out of scope, as the critical evaluation already recommends (`critical-evaluation.md:21-33`). No browser benchmark or runtime performance improvement is claimed by this documentation review.
