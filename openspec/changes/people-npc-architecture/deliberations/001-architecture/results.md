# Deliberation Summary

## Context

- **Task**: Plan a structural refactor of festival people and NPC systems so ownership, activity, rendering, interactions, and extension paths are coherent. Freeze behavior is a motivating observation, not the objective.
- **Personas Consulted**: Architect, Auditor, Pragmatist, Profiler, Adversary, and Mediator.
- **Date**: 2026-10-25.
- **Mode**: Tier 3 synthesis. All five independent positions completed; there was no cross-examination round.
- **Status**: Planning only. This recommendation does not authorize runtime changes, implementation, commits, or deployment. No runtime tests or performance measurements were performed by this council.
- **Review boundary**: The immutable [input-plan](input-plan/design.md) preserves the draft the personas reviewed. Their abbreviated plan citations refer to that snapshot. The completed [independent Astra review](../../astra-review.md) is included as a separately attributed supplemental review. It did not read the council positions and is not a sixth council persona or a cross-examination round.

**TL/DR:** Proceed with a small controller-level runtime over existing storage, but prove two contrasting production paths early: Healing Arts as an activity borrowing a crowd person and its rendering authority, then children as an independent population. Astra identified a concrete model-to-simulation dependency in Healing Arts, which makes that ownership slice the stronger first proof. Complete the remaining migration through the same boundaries while preserving the exact frame barriers that affect smiles, scoring, collision, and render publication.

### Change Group 2: Two early proofs, then complete the migration

**Scope**: Establish a minimal public contract through real consumers, then migrate every covered population, group, activity, and relevant vehicle/place adapter.

**Estimated Effort**: 16–24 focused sittings, divided into independently reviewable production slices.

**Dependencies**: Group 1. Within this group, finish the bounded Healing Arts ownership slice and then the child population slice before broad rollout. This deliberately revises the Pragmatist’s child-first recommendation in light of Astra’s verified reverse motion dependency.

**Tasks**:

1. Introduce the thin runtime at existing call slots, with registration-time capability validation and precompiled dispatch/provider lists. The runtime imports no concrete population controller, creates no independent RAF/timer/visibility loop, and receives the accepted main-loop delta. Preserve nested scheduling until its own replacement removes the prior call. Sources: `input-plan/design.md:56-83`, `input-plan/design.md:104-130`, [Adversary, clock finding](council-adversary.md), and [Profiler, Priority Sequence](council-profiler.md).
2. Define lifetime-safe handles with one runtime-issued registration-incarnation token in addition to slot generation. Replacing a controller under its logical key cannot revive old handles or releases. Allocate identity only on lifetime creation, document child relocation identity, and audit external holders. Lost-smile effects must preserve live tracking while the person exists and use an explicit last-valid-position fallback after removal, without following a replacement occupant. Sources: `input-plan/design.md:87-102`, `src/smiles.js:81-93`, `src/smiles.js:125-128`; shared finding in [Architect](council-architect.md), [Auditor](council-auditor.md), and [Adversary](council-adversary.md).
3. Specify authority transitions and ordered, reentrancy-safe teardown: the population validates person and eligibility, resource owners validate reservations, and acquisition either commits completely or rolls back only its tentative work while preserving the prior activity on failure. Revoke an outgoing claim’s write authority before incoming authority begins; validate tokens on writes/ticks as well as release. The current claim selects the existing movement algorithm, while group intent remains a lower-priority proposal and the owner defines resume policy. For teardown, stop scheduling and new acquisitions; revoke the current authority token before release callbacks; release borrowers while required metadata and fallback renderers remain valid; detach owned bindings and spatial facets; then dispose owned GPU/audio resources. Repeated callbacks must neither release a later claim nor restore a binding after final removal. Keep actual person removal with its population owner while allowing groups to request collective removal. Sources: `src/chunks.js:572-597`, `src/healingArtsActivity.js:40-50`, `src/monks.js:168-172`, [Architect, Structural Risks](council-architect.md), [Adversary, Priority Sequence](council-adversary.md), [Astra, Findings 1 and 6](../../astra-review.md).

Complete Healing Arts as the first vertical slice, implementing only the public crowd commands, claim-checked movement implementations, exclusive activity claims, and render-binding operations this real consumer requires. Preserve spawning/adoption, identity, eligibility, cooldown semantics, dusk/dawn behavior, and restoration on release. Extract the existing trajectory and person/pole constraint algorithm from the model into an activity-owned sampler and pose cache. Preserve the bootstrap fallback, the activity’s consumption of the previous sample, and subsequent sampling only at the existing near/night-transition barrier; do not run an unconditional current-sample update. Model writes become consumers of that cache, including when render uploads are skipped. Test real tent unload and eliminate private renderer calls before propagating the contract. Sources: `src/healingArtsActivity.js:21-50`, `src/healingArtsActivity.js:124-126`, `src/models/healingArts.js:193-237`, `src/models/healingArts.js:267`, `src/chunks.js:68-74`, [Astra, Findings 1 and 3](../../astra-review.md).

Keep adult Crowd behind its existing storage/render adapter. Prove a test-only independent population can register without new character-specific main-loop, honk, collision, inspection, or teardown branches. This preserves the Pragmatist’s independent-population proof before broad rollout while first addressing the harder authority boundary. Sources: `src/obstacles.js:389-454`, `src/obstacles.js:495-514`, [Pragmatist, Slice 1](council-pragmatist.md), [Astra, Findings 3 and 5](../../astra-review.md).

Keep registry-first collision order, localized broadphases, scratch pools, instanced batches, packed accessory counts, distance gates, and independent nightness polling.
