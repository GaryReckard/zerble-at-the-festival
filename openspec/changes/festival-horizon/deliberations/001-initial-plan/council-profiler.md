## Profiler's Position

### Priority Sequence

1. **Measure before geometry.** Capture fixed-pose off-state scene draws, submitted triangles, programs, resources, and frame timing on low/mid/high, then microbenchmark the complete cold horizon boundary job against the existing 3/4/5ms walls. The renderer HUD exists specifically to establish this truth first (`AGENTS.md:223-228`; `src/perf.js:88-90,116-118,134-136`).
2. **Turn “bounded” into exact tier contracts.** Pin primitive segment counts, instance capacities, road vertex/index capacity, maximum attribute-upload bytes, and submitted-triangle ceilings before writing the renderer. A six-draw architecture is promising, but the global batches' triangle cost is otherwise unknown.
3. **Prove the yieldable data path.** Implement deterministic selection and road clipping in a pure focused harness, including a genuinely cold cache. If one `queryRegion()` call crosses the tier wall, restructure the query into deterministic strips/cells before integrating Three.js; do not defer only the work that happens after the monolithic call.
4. **Implement the cheapest batches incrementally.** Start with the road, stage canopy, and roof peaks behind `?farField=1`; use fixed owned buffers, opaque unlit materials, no shadows/lights, and correct bounds recomputation. Add trusses and the two night-marker batches only while the measured triangle and frame deltas remain within their caps.
5. **Add handoff after the static budget is green.** Keep the Bayer fade proxy-only and opaque, but instrument per-instance attribute uploads, use bounded update ranges, and prove a stable shader-program plateau through repeated ownership transitions.
6. **Integrate under one streaming wall.** Run chunk completion first, then horizon observation, but account both systems against one per-frame generation allowance. Preserve the previous snapshot until planning **and application/upload preparation** are ready, rather than timing only plan discovery.
7. **Promote only from repeated full-game A/B/A evidence.** Verify Noon/Midnight, all tiers, mobile, reduced motion, long travel, and unload, while recording absolute totals as well as deltas. The existing default-off gate is the correct rollback boundary (`openspec/changes/festival-horizon/tasks.md:55-72`).

### Performance-Sound Decisions Already Present

- The proposed six global batches keep the expected draw delta near +4 by day and +6 by night, rather than scaling draws with hub count (`openspec/changes/festival-horizon/design.md:59-75`).
- `MeshBasicMaterial`, opaque depth-writing Bayer discard, no real lights, and no shadow casters avoid the project's largest steady-state multipliers and transparent sorting (`openspec/changes/festival-horizon/design.md:77-80,102-120`).
- Fixed-capacity nearest-candidate retention and in-place buffer rewrites are the right allocation model, provided the apply/upload frame is budgeted and resources actually plateau (`openspec/changes/festival-horizon/design.md:70-75,155-160`).
- The experiment remains default-off until long-travel and tier gates pass, so an unsuccessful visual-depth spend does not become a production regression (`openspec/changes/festival-horizon/design.md:122-128,167-176`).

### Budget Estimate

-   **Draw delta**: Expected **+4 scene draws at Noon** and **+6 at Midnight** if the two marker batches are actually hidden by day; hard ceiling +12. The closest tier is low. Against the latest real-device sample, the illustrative enabled total would be roughly 871-873 draws, but a fixed-pose same-state A/B is still required because the 867-draw sample was a travel capture rather than this horizon route (`openspec/changes/festival-horizon/design.md:59-68,115-120`; `openspec/changes/perf-pass-4/session-log.md:254-260`).
-   **Triangle delta**: **Not bounded numerically by the current artifacts.** Fixed instance counts are not enough because pool capacities, primitive topology, and road index capacity are left as measurement outputs (`openspec/changes/festival-horizon/design.md:70-75,178-181`). Before renderer work, pin conservative promotion caps of **no more than +5k submitted triangles on low and +10k on mid/high**, while also reporting the absolute enabled total against 150k/400k/1.2M. Those marginal caps are feasible for box/prism silhouettes and a two-triangle-per-segment ribbon, but they must be proven rather than assumed.
-   **Cost type**: **Both.** Boundary queries, sorting, snapshot construction, pool rewrite, bounds recomputation, and GPU uploads are allocation/spawn-stall risks; the six visible batches, their horizon-wide culling granularity, and active fade uploads are steady-state or transient-frame GPU risks.
-   **Low/mid-tier verdict**: **At risk until the plan adds explicit triangle/upload/culling gates.** Instancing and opaque unlit materials are the correct foundation, and shadows/lights stay at +0, but the latest low-tier real-device evidence is already above the nominal HUD budgets. The feature can proceed behind `?farField=1`; default promotion requires a small measured marginal cost and no felt regression rather than claiming the current whole game is suddenly within the historical absolute targets.

### Required Plan Changes

1. Add a pre-renderer benchmark immediately after Task 1.1 that clears or bypasses relevant memo caches and measures the complete cold boundary job on representative dense seeds. It must time `queryRegion`, every `festivalPlan` read, deterministic nearest selection, road clipping, matrix/color/fade population, bounding-volume recomputation, and the next rendered frame's buffer upload separately. The current Task 3.3 measures too late and names only descriptor planning (`openspec/changes/festival-horizon/tasks.md:38-40`).
2. Make incremental queryability a design requirement, not a conditional implementation wish. If the monolithic `queryRegion()` call breaches the wall, query deterministic strips/cells or expose a yieldable public iterator; slicing work after that call returns cannot recover the frame (`src/worldgen/index.js:79-89`; `src/worldgen/roads.js:312-336`).
3. Add exact per-tier capacity, vertex, index, and submitted-triangle maxima to Task 1.2 and the bounded-rendering requirement. Include a pure estimator in `bin/test-far-field`, and fail when the configured maxima exceed those caps. The existing spec constrains draws but gives triangles no number (`openspec/changes/festival-horizon/specs/festival-horizon/spec.md:43-60`).
4. Expand Task 3.3 so the **entire** pending snapshot and apply path shares one wall with ordinary chunk streaming. A FarField job and `ChunkManager.update()` must not each consume an independent 3/4/5ms allowance in the same frame. Budget pool rewrite and GPU submission explicitly, and split the apply phase too if it misses the wall (`ARCHITECTURE.md:357-360`; `openspec/changes/festival-horizon/design.md:97-100`).
5. Add a culling contract to Task 2.1: recompute bounding spheres after every transform/road rewrite and measure global-batch submitted triangles with the camera facing away from most proxies. If low exceeds its triangle cap, partition only the offending geometry into a small fixed number of spatial buckets while staying under the 12-draw ceiling.
6. Add attribute-upload instrumentation to Task 3.2. Fade buffers should use bounded update ranges when supported, and runtime material state must change uniforms/data only, never shader source or defines. Verify program count plateaus after the first Noon/Midnight/handoff cycle.
7. Strengthen Task 5.4 to record both delta and absolute enabled draws/triangles for low/mid/high, plus `p95`/worst frame at the boundary frame and the following upload frame. “No meaningful regression” should be operationalized as **no repeatable >1ms p95 frame-time increase while parked and no boundary frame beyond the tier wall attributable to FarField**; if device noise makes 1ms inconclusive, use repeated A/B/A runs and retain default-off.

### Verdict

-   **Verdict**: Proceed with mitigations
-   **Key Concern**: The plan slices descriptor work only after a potentially monolithic worldgen query and then applies every GPU-buffer rewrite atomically, so it can create a new boundary hitch even though its steady-state draw count is small.
-   **Recommendation**: Keep the experiment behind `?farField=1` and proceed after folding in exact per-tier triangle/upload caps, a yieldable cold-query path, shared chunk-plus-horizon frame budgeting, explicit bounds/update-range handling, and absolute-plus-delta A/B gates. The six-batch, no-light, no-shadow renderer is a sound bounded prototype once those omissions are closed.
