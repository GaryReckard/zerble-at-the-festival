## Adversary's Position

### Priority Sequence

1. Amend the experiment contract before renderer work: the horizon must no-op under `?worldgen=0`, consume cached worldgen arrays without mutation, and observe chunk completion through a narrow predicate rather than receiving the mutable `ChunkManager.loaded` map. The current design declares a read-only reference but JavaScript does not enforce that boundary (`design.md:47-56`; `src/chunks.js:306,336-337`).
2. Centralize the descriptor-to-owner calculation and key format, then lock the exact negative-boundary cases in the focused test. `placeChunkProps` currently embeds its half-open test locally, while the loaded-map key is separately private to `chunks.js`; copying both rules into `farField.js` creates two opportunities for a handoff that works at positive coordinates but fails at `-40`, `-120`, or on a key-format change (`src/worldgen/placement.js:24-31`; `src/chunks.js:384,644-646`).
3. Prove the planner as a versioned, non-preemptive state machine before adding GPU pools. A newer player-cell request must cancel or supersede an older pending snapshot, and every indivisible call such as `queryRegion` or one cold `festivalPlan` must fit the far-field wall. The plan cannot promise to split work after an already-completed synchronous call has caused the hitch (`design.md:84-100`; `tasks.md:38-40`).
4. Build the six fixed pools with explicit rewrite invariants: active counts, matrix/color/fade update flags, recomputed bounds, stable tie-breaks, and owner-only idempotent disposal. Then test a second rewrite and a second dispose, not merely initial construction (`design.md:59-80`; `src/chunks.js:591-604`).
5. Integrate after `ChunkManager.update` behind the default-off and worldgen-v2 gates, initialize already-loaded proxies directly to hidden, and preserve the title tap to `Sound.init()` adjacency exactly. The real title backdrop is built before that tap, so initial horizon planning is also boot-path work even though it is render-only (`src/world.js:47-73`; `src/main.js:422,648-651`; `src/sound.js:219-230`).
6. Verify the known unhappy paths in order: proxy-only Noon/Midnight, exact road elevation, low-tier real reload, negative chunk boundaries, rapid multi-cell teleport, load then unload then reload of the same owner, reduced motion toggled during a handoff, hidden-tab continuation, legacy `?worldgen=0`, Safari/WebKit shader compilation, and finally long-travel off/on measurements. Promotion remains conditional on all gates, as the existing plan correctly requires (`tasks.md:55-72`).

### Vulnerabilities Found

-   **The v1 escape hatch would render a false v2 horizon**: `USE_WORLDGEN_V2` still supports `?worldgen=0` (`src/perf.js:61-73`), but the proposed enablement is only `?farField=1` (`design.md:122-128`). In that combination, full chunks use the legacy theme path while the horizon reads v2 hearts, plans, and arterials. The proxy roads cannot align with the loaded roads, and loaded legacy chunk keys would hide unrelated v2 proxies. Make effective enablement `farFieldRequested && USE_WORLDGEN_V2`, add a focused no-op assertion, and boot that URL before promotion. — Severity: Critical
-   **“Read-only” inputs are mutable shared objects**: `festivalPlan` returns the array held in `_seamedCache` (`src/worldgen/festival.js:820-830`), and road results carry cached arterial point arrays (`src/worldgen/roads.js:247-280,312-332`). Sorting candidate descriptors, clipping points in place, annotating descriptors with visibility, or reusing those arrays as writable buffer staging would mutate the generator's cached truth and could change later real-chunk construction order despite consuming no new RNG. The tests must deep-freeze inputs or hash them before and after every rebuild, and the implementation must create its own compact candidate records. The same principle favors an `isLoaded(owner)` predicate over the mutable map proposed at `design.md:49-56`. — Severity: High
-   **The proposed budget is additive, and some work cannot be preempted**: `ChunkManager.update` can spend the entire `PERF.chunkBudgetMs` before returning (`src/chunks.js:347-352`), after which the design gives the horizon “the same” 3/4/5ms wall (`design.md:97-100`). That can turn a 3ms low-tier streaming wall into roughly 6ms before rendering, while a single synchronous `queryRegion` or cold `festivalPlan` cannot be retroactively sliced. Use a shared frame deadline or a distinct remaining-time budget, version pending jobs, measure the maximum indivisible step, and fail promotion if one atomic worldgen call alone exceeds the wall. A rapid teleport test must prove an older pending snapshot can never commit over a newer cell. — Severity: High
-   **The road underlay can repeat the already-documented “under the grass” failure**: The task says only “narrower and lower” (`tasks.md:19-21`). The authoritative v2 road is explicitly raised to `y=0.06` (`src/chunks.js:958-991`), and Gary's prior correction records that a road material/path combination rendered invisibly under the recentered ground until the runtime and depth behavior was changed (`src/chunks.js:877-899`, commit `db4e0ef5`). “Lower” must mean a tested elevation strictly above the ground and below `0.06`, with compatible polygon offset/depth behavior. Add grazing-angle approach and recentered-ground travel captures, because a top-down hub view will not expose this failure. — Severity: High
-   **Handoff ownership is specified by duplication, not by one source of truth**: The half-open predicate exists only as an inner function in `placeChunkProps` (`src/worldgen/placement.js:24-31`), while `chunkKey` is private and comma-delimited in `chunks.js` (`src/chunks.js:644-646`). The proposed `ownerKey` therefore has to recreate both details. Export a pure owner-coordinate helper and expose loaded state through `ChunkManager.isLoaded(cx, cz)` or a world-owned predicate. Test exact boundaries on both axes, including negative half-cell ties, then load, unload, and reload the same owner twice. — Severity: High
-   **Rewritten pools can be culled using stale bounds**: The design calls out matrix/color `needsUpdate` (`design.md:70-75`) but not `InstancedMesh.boundingSphere` or the road geometry's bounding sphere. Both pools move their active contents hundreds of metres on an atomic rewrite. If bounds were computed for the previous snapshot, a valid new horizon can vanish only at particular camera angles or cells. Recompute bounds after every committed rewrite, or intentionally disable frustum culling and account for that cost. The focused test should assert the bounds enclose all active instances and road vertices after a second distant rebuild. — Severity: High
-   **The hub sandbox's live tier selector cannot prove the real tier path**: `PERF` is selected once at module load (`src/perf.js:56-75`), and `threeShim` also freezes its low-tier swap decision at module evaluation (`src/threeShim.js:22-23,46-66`). A control that merely injects low capacities into an already-high page is a useful composition preview, but it is not a low-tier boot or shader test. Label it as simulated configuration, and make the real low/mid/high checks page reloads using `?perf=`. A WebKit/Safari render that compiles the injected `onBeforeCompile` shader is required because a static string test and Chromium cannot falsify the Safari-only class of failures documented in `threeShim.js:12-20`. — Severity: High
-   **Initial construction can add a boot hitch or a ghost handoff before accessibility state is ready**: `buildWorld` synchronously preloads chunks before the title card is used (`src/world.js:47-73`; `src/main.js:413-425`), whereas `A11y.init()` runs later and is the point that resolves persisted reduced motion (`src/main.js:609-616`; `src/a11y.js:18-30`). If `FarField` builds its first plan synchronously there, it lengthens first paint; if it initializes fades as visible, already-loaded clusters can briefly dither over real geometry; and if it reads reduced motion during construction, it sees the default false. Initial ownership must snap without animation, initial planning time must be measured separately from boundary rebuilds, and live updates must read the resolved setting after `A11y.init()`. — Severity: Medium
-   **Owner-only disposal can be defeated by sandbox nesting**: `disposeChunkByKey` traverses a hub group and disposes every non-shared mesh plus each `InstancedMesh` buffer (`src/chunks.js:591-604`). If the sandbox nests the FarField group under the current hub and then also calls `FarField.dispose()`, resources are disposed through two owners. Keep the peer group outside the hub-preview group, have exactly one teardown path call `FarField.dispose()`, and test repeated hub rebuilds plus double-call idempotence. — Severity: Medium
-   **The default-off flag limits behavior, not module-load blast radius**: Static import from `world.js` still evaluates `farField.js` on every boot even when the experiment is off. A top-level exception, a frozen-module mutation, or a shader/material side effect can still kill the title card before the flag is consulted. The off-path acceptance test must therefore load the actual main page on all tiers and assert zero FarField allocations and unchanged renderer programs, not merely assert that no FarField instance was constructed (`threeShim.js:12-20`; `spec.md:148-153`). — Severity: Medium

### Verdict

-   **Verdict**: Proceed with mitigations
-   **Key Concern**: The proposal treats cached descriptor arrays and `ChunkManager.loaded` as read-only without enforcing that boundary, so one innocent in-place sort or annotation can alter the real world while every RNG call remains untouched.
-   **Recommendation**: Amend the design and tasks before implementation with the v2-only gate, defensive non-mutating descriptor consumption, a centralized owner predicate, versioned pending rebuilds under a shared deadline, explicit road elevation and bounds refresh rules, and real reload-based tier/WebKit checks. With those mitigations, the render-only scope is bounded enough to proceed.
