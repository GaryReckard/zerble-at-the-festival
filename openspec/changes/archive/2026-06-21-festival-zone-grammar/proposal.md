## Why

Two playtest rounds called the procedural festival "a jumbled mess" — trucks clipping vendor rows, porta-potties scattered at random, a drum circle spawned *inside* a food-truck ring, the arch beside the dancefloor instead of out on the road. The `worldgen-layout-harness` change proved this is **one root cause, not N bugs**: `festival.js` plans each hub as *points with scalar clear-radii* (`KIND_FOOTPRINT`) while the `chunks.js` builders construct *oriented shapes that exceed those radii*, so `resolveOverlaps` settles clusters at separations that guarantee clipping; the dancefloor rect repels only trees; builders place sub-components blind; and nothing enforces stage-to-stage distance across hubs. The harness also built the gate: `verification/baseline.md` records **106 error / 92 warn** across 10 seeds (worst clip: a booth inside a truck by 7.5 m). This change is the fix — and now is the time because the gate (linter + baseline + hub viewer + overlay) just landed and `DEFAULT_WORLDGEN_V2` is still flag-off, so we fix the jumble *before* it ships to players.

## What Changes

- **Builders express sub-component layout as pure data** (D-C′, deferred here from the harness): each `chunks.js` worldgen builder splits into a pure `layout(rng, env) → records[]` half (positions/radii/yaw, no three.js) and a `buildMesh(records)` half. Crowd gets **pre-rolled params** (fixes today's tier-dependent layouts — `crowd.spawn` draws from the cluster rng with a tier-sized pool). The dry-run env widens to `{ waterAt, blockedAt }`. `Math.random()` sites in `buildStage` are transcribed as-is, never folded into the seeded stream.
- **Descriptors carry oriented bounding shapes** derived from the *same* `FESTIVAL_TUNING` constants the builder uses (via the harness's `clusterExtent`/analytic helpers, promoted to true per-kind extents), and overlap / road / water / dancefloor checks run against **shapes, not scalar radii**.
- **Zone slotting replaces scatter-then-relax**: per hub, the planner reserves non-overlapping zones — stage + a **hard-reserved front wedge** on the front axis F (against *all* placement, not just trees); vendor aisles **straddling roads** with full **camps auto-reserved behind**; food courts **off-road at a minimum stage distance** with an optional **mini spur road** to the court center; the large LEAF drum circle in a **forest clearing with an access path** wide enough to drive in; potties **attached to a parent zone's edge, facing it**; the spawn **arch out on the road as a threshold** before the stage scene.
- **Cross-hub `stage-spacing`** becomes a real placement constraint (today there is none). **Bubble vendors become a tunable probability** (today: guaranteed 1/hub — too many). **Sugar Shacks** become a percentage of food-truck clusters.
- **Per-sub-component `registry` clearance restored** in builders as the graceful-degradation backstop (main's theme builders had it; v2 dropped it).
- The change **moves the POI golden once, deliberately**, re-records it, re-verifies node==browser, and regenerates the `?worldgen=1` world. `DEFAULT_WORLDGEN_V2` flip stays a **separate, later** step (Gary-sequenced).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `festival-zone-grammar`: the hub-scale planner that slots non-overlapping oriented zones (stage+front wedge, road-straddling vendor aisles with camps behind, off-road courts + spur, forest-clearing drum + path, attached potties, threshold arch), enforces cross-hub stage spacing, and makes bubble-vendor/sugar-shack presence probabilistic — graded against `verification/baseline.md`.
- `builder-layout-extraction`: the pure-data split of the `chunks.js` worldgen builders (`layout(rng,env)→records` / `buildMesh(records)`), crowd pre-rolled params, `env={waterAt,blockedAt}` injection, and the restored per-sub-component registry-clearance backstop — the substrate the grammar plans on, landing under this change's moving golden.

## Impact

- **Subsystems**: world generation (`src/worldgen/festival.js` planner), the `chunks.js` worldgen builders (`buildWorldgenKind` + `buildStage`/`buildVendorRowAt`/`buildFoodCourtAt`/`buildCampVillageAt`/potty/drum/bubble builders), `src/crowd.js` (pre-rolled spawn params), and `src/models/*` builders touched by the layout/mesh split.
- **Tripwires brushed** (deliberation expected — the determinism + boot-order + lifecycle signatures all fire by design): **determinism** — the POI golden moves once, deliberately, and `?worldgen=1` regenerates; gated by the harness snapshot diff + draw-count canary + node==browser re-verify, one builder per commit. **Per-tier perf budget** — zone slotting must not add draws (verify the backtick panel both tiers). **Disposal/`userData.shared`** — extracted builders keep pooled-resource tagging. **importmap** — no new modules expected; if any, all four html files + `bin/check-importmaps`.
- **Player-visible**: yes (the whole point) — CHANGELOG required per commit; success is numeric (baseline burndown) AND Gary-judged (in-game 3D screenshots via the hub viewer + playtest markers).
- **Scope check**: the "two owners, do NOT merge" constant pairs the harness D8 deliberately left split (planner's `DANCEFLOOR_DEPTH_BASE` vs `buildStage`'s internal 9 m; the legacy `buildFoodPlaza`/`buildVendorRow`/`buildCampVillage` twins) become **legal and wanted merges here**, where world drift is expected and gated. Every builder that places sub-components blind is in scope for the registry-clearance restore (documented per-builder in tasks).
