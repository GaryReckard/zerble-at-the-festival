## TL;DR

A rider who climbs aboard sometimes floats a thought bubble showing where they want to go: a stage, the food trucks, a drum circle, their camp, the beach. A compass strip, the festival map, a brightening bubble, humming and short remarks guide the player there. Stopping near the destination ends the ride with a thumbs-up and a burst of smiles. Three variants ride on the same system: an urgent porta-potty run, a mountaintop request, and a monk party that travels as one group.

## The Problem

Zerble has a whole procedural festival and a cart full of passengers, but riders never want anything. Rides end on a timer, so the player has no reason to drive toward any particular place. The design has been waiting in `.claude/passenger-quests-design.md` since the summer and Festival Run reserved a scoring door for it.

## Proposed Fix

- **Destinations come from the festival plan, not from loaded scenery.** The worldgen plan knows where every stage, court, market, drum circle and camp is, even when that area has not streamed in yet. Beaches, hammocks and porta-potties come from the live registry.
- **A small pure quest engine** (`src/quests/`) owns picking, progress, hints, missed-it detection, patience and rewards. It has no three.js or DOM, so Node tests cover it.
- **Crowd gets a narrow passenger API** (read a rider's head position, hold a ride open, end a ride with a celebration). The quest engine never reaches into NPC records.
- **Presentation is layered and optional:** thought-bubble sprite, compass strip (on by default, with a setting), map pins through the existing `setObjectives`, a proximity hum, and toasts.
- **Rewards are ordinary smile pickups**, so combo, Lurleen doubling and the leaderboard's raw-smile accounting work unchanged in both modes.

The technical detail, constants and file map are in [design.md](design.md). **A new agent should read [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) first.**

## Key Decisions

- Gary chose the full stack, counted in Free Roam and Festival Run, with a light OpenSpec record and a skipped council (2026-11-15). The work lives on the `passenger-quests` branch.
- Quest rolls use `Math.random`, the same as boarding. No seeded world stream is read or advanced, so worlds do not change.
- Quest riders never time out on the ordinary ride timer. Patience is a stall timer that only runs while the player is not getting closer, so chilling with a passenger close to their goal is still fine.
- The timed porta-potty run is the single deliberate exception to "no countdowns". It is rare, short and costs at most two smiles.
- At most three quests are active at once, so the compass strip stays readable.

## Risks & Watch-outs

- **Leaderboard plausibility.** Quest smiles count as raw smiles. The reward table is sized so quests add well under the Worker's placeholder ceiling, but the ceiling itself is still uncalibrated (see ROADMAP "Festival Run acceptance").
- **Crowd lifecycle.** A rider can be scattered, bail on an empty tank, or lose their seat. Every exit path must cancel the quest and free the bubble sprite.
- **Performance freeze.** A device performance session is pending on another branch of work. This change adds at most five sprites and three DOM chips, switches itself off under the performance lab, device autopilot and timed captures, and adds no per-frame registry scans.
- **Not doing:** kids requesting rides, tokens, streaks or a tip jar.
