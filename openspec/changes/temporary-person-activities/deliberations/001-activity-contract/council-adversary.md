### Vulnerabilities Found

- **Claimed adults skip passive danger.** Crowd returns before trajectory detection when an external activity owns the person (`src/crowd.js:953`, `src/crowd.js:1120`). Passing full cart state only to the activity does not resolve competing movement authority. Revoke or explicitly suspend activity writes before the existing dodge runs, including activity ticks later in the same frame. Severity: Critical.
- **The existing claim API destroys the old activity on interruption.** Replacing or removing a claim invokes the release callback and every registered resource cleanup (`src/people/activityClaims.js:24`, `src/people/activityClaims.js:59`, `src/crowd.js:2545`). Resumption cannot rely on a stale claim or a reservation already released; either reacquire after validating the person, venue and resources, or introduce an explicitly tested temporary authority protocol that preserves those invariants. Test failed preparation and reentrant removal. Severity: High.
- **Hidden rigs suppress honk reactions.** Honk skips hidden activity bindings (`src/crowd.js:2522`), and Healing Arts hides the ordinary body while its rig is active (`src/healingArtsActivity.js:51`). Blindly removing that guard also affects genuinely hidden or physically constrained people, so distinguish a substituting visible rig from a potty occupant or rider and restore one body before moving. Severity: High.
- **Appearance is not represented fully by the current public view.** The adult view exposes shirt, skin and scale (`src/crowd.js:2160`), whereas independently built hoop and frisbee models choose their own shirt, skin and hair (`src/models/hulaHooper.js:33`, `src/models/hulaHooper.js:132`, `src/models/frisbeePlayer.js:46`, `src/models/frisbeePlayer.js:160`). Copying only shirt and skin changes identity on transition. Preserve the existing adult binding or define and verify a complete appearance transfer, including hair and patterned clothing; Gary's appearance correction in commit `ea3cecf` is relevant history. Severity: High.
- **A paired session can retain a stale catcher or chase into danger.** The existing frisbee simulation directly advances the catcher toward the predicted disc location (`src/people/frisbees.js:197`, `src/people/frisbees.js:228`). Interrupt both roles coherently, stop catcher movement immediately, and explicitly settle held, flying and landed discs without teleporting either participant. A missing partner, place or claim must terminate or safely reacquire the whole session. Severity: High.
- **Venue recycling is not person lifetime management.** The hooper pool hides and relocates performers when their attractor disappears or moves out of range (`src/people/hoopers.js:132`), while ordinary crowd people have their own lifetime and collision publication. Migrating that behavior literally could teleport a borrowed person, duplicate bodies or colliders, or strand a reservation after unload. Keep venue recycling separate from the person's lifetime and test second unload plus handle reuse. Severity: High.
- **An urgent reaction is not always a free walk.** Riders are excluded from honk flight and a blocked seat departure can refuse a dodge (`src/crowd.js:1123`, `src/crowd.js:2530`); potty states have dedicated handling (`src/crowd.js:1057`). Preserve those physical constraints and explicit eligibility instead of applying an unconditional flee transition to all handles. Severity: High.
- **Late updates can invalidate same-frame collision or scoring.** Adult rendering, score collection, roaming activities and late Healing Arts execute at distinct barriers (`openspec/changes/people-npc-architecture/dependency-slots.md:11`). Exercise the real schedule and verify one authoritative collider throughout transitions, rather than moving scheduling for convenience. Severity: High.
- **Cross-cutting boot and lifecycle regressions hide outside fixtures.** Preserve shared-resource disposal, fresh RNG salts and unchanged seeded draw order (`AGENTS.md`); the Three.js shim exists to prevent Safari module namespace assignment crashes (`src/threeShim.js:12`), and the gesture handler synchronously initializes the three-stage iOS audio unlock (`src/main.js:973`, `src/sound.js:271`). A real muted game boot and production-backed fixtures are required, while physical iOS audio remains a separate device check. Severity: Medium.

### Verdict

- **Verdict**: Proceed with mitigations
- **Key Concern**: An interrupted activity must lose movement authority before any later activity write, without confusing released reservations with resumable ones.
- **Recommendation**: Establish and test the single-person handoff against the production schedule first, then extend the same proven authority and cancellation rules to coordinated pairs and dance.
