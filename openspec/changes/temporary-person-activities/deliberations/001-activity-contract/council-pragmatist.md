## Pragmatist’s Position

### Critical Path

The first dependency is a production-backed fixture that exposes an existing Crowd person, a real activity reservation, the cart’s position, heading and speed, and both the published body and collider. The extension example already exercises public person claims and place adapters (`openspec/changes/people-npc-architecture/extension-guide.md:6`, `openspec/changes/people-npc-architecture/extension-guide.md:26`); the existing people workshop runs production controllers rather than a replacement simulation (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, “People ownership and composition”). Extend that surface to drive the complete hula cycle before changing both pair and dance controllers.

The blocking contract is authority during interruption. Existing `ActivityClaims` revokes an old claim and runs its owned resource cleanup on replacement, so a suspended session cannot simply keep using that claim (`src/people/activityClaims.js:11`, `src/people/activityClaims.js:39`, `src/people/activityClaims.js:61`). Define a narrowly scoped suspension/resumption protocol that invalidates movement writes while retaining or deliberately releasing a separately validated reservation; do not retain a stale claim or let an activity reacquire movement while danger persists. Crowd's normal trajectory check occurs after several early-return activity paths (`src/crowd.js:992`, `src/crowd.js:1017`, `src/crowd.js:1057`, `src/crowd.js:1121`), and honk currently skips hidden activity bindings (`src/crowd.js:2523`). Both routes must reach a common, physically appropriate reaction decision before an activity can publish its next pose.

### Priority Sequence

1. Characterize current hooper placement, appearance, RNG, collision publication and the pair's held/flying/landed transitions with the production owners and existing tests (`bin/test-hoopers-ownership`, `bin/test-frisbees-ownership`). Record the actual game frame slots and scoring order before integration (`openspec/changes/people-npc-architecture/dependency-slots.md:10`). Preserve existing streams rather than drawing new selection randomness from constructors or world generation (`AGENTS.md`, “Constraints that must survive edits”).
2. Add an explicitly eligible adult-to-hula path and a fixture for ordinary → hula → approaching/honking cart → dodge → validated resume → finish and walk. Reuse `laneDodgeTest`, `laneDodgeDir`, `laneDodgeUrgency` and `honkScatterParams` (`src/steering.js:34`, `src/steering.js:54`, `src/steering.js:75`, `src/steering.js:92`). Verify stale activity pose writes fail throughout the reaction, the same handle and appearance survive, and exactly one body and collider exist at each transition.
3. Migrate the hooper experience without leaving a second independently owned person or collider behind. The current hooper pool owns its own identities, roaming motion and active-place collision (`src/people/hoopers.js:30`, `src/people/hoopers.js:47`, `openspec/changes/people-npc-architecture/acceptance.md:24`). Replace or adapt its ownership deliberately; registering its existing handle beside a borrowed adult would duplicate the participant.
4. Apply the proven handoff to a paired session, acquiring both people and resources atomically or rolling back partial preparation. A pair owns its disc and currently moves the catcher during both flight and pickup (`src/people/frisbees.js:105`, `src/people/frisbees.js:190`, `src/people/frisbees.js:259`). Specify and test interruption for held, flying and landed states, stale partner/place/resource invalidation, either participant disappearing, and both valid resumption and cancellation. Never chase a disc during an urgent reaction.
5. Apply the same contract to Healing Arts dancing, retaining its explicit adult selection, rig reservation, prior-sample cadence and place lifetime (`src/healingArtsActivity.js:29`, `src/healingArtsActivity.js:37`, `openspec/changes/people-npc-architecture/extension-guide.md:136`). Check protected potty, seated and vehicle states independently, because a physical reaction is not always an immediate flee (`src/crowd.js:1017`, `src/crowd.js:1057`, `src/crowd.js:2533`).
6. Run targeted ownership and interaction tests plus `npm run check`, then verify the production-backed fixture and the actual game with the title-card gesture, movement, honk, collision, streaming and console checks. Preserve original frame barriers, independent nightness and synchronous audio startup (`AGENTS.md`, “Run and verify” and “Constraints that must survive edits”). Compare visual appearance at noon and midnight and relevant tier budgets if new rendering changes them. Respect local browser process ownership and teardown (`AGENTS.override.md`, “Browser and process safety”).

### Deferred / Park on ROADMAP

- Broader population enrollment: children, formation members, wooks and vehicle riders have distinct owners or do not expose the adult activity facade (`openspec/changes/people-npc-architecture/extension-guide.md:139`). They do not block the requested adult hula, frisbee and dance cycles, and should only be added through deliberate compatibility and eligibility policies. This is a scope boundary, not a deferral of any requested activity.
- A new universal scheduler or population rewrite: existing frame slots and public authority boundaries are sufficient entry points (`openspec/changes/people-npc-architecture/dependency-slots.md:4`, `openspec/changes/people-npc-architecture/extension-guide.md:94`). No requested behavior depends on replacing them.

### Incremental Delivery Plan

- **Slice 1 (ship first):** Production-backed adult hula fixture and real-game integration, including explicit hula eligibility, reservation validation, safe interruption, resumption and departure, identity/appearance continuity, and singular body/collider. Verify both passive charge and honk, invalidation on removal/unload, and the complete requested cycle in the fixture and live game.
- **Slice 2 (ship after):** Paired frisbee and Healing Arts dance use the same tested authority handoff. Verify both partner acquisition rollback and all disc states, plus dancing at the existing nightness and frame barriers. Repeat game boot and exercise the integrated activities; a sandbox result alone cannot establish streaming and collision correctness (`AGENTS.md`, “Run and verify”).

### Verdict

- **Verdict**: Proceed with mitigations
- **Key Concern**: A later activity update can overwrite a valid dodge unless suspension invalidates its movement authority before the activity's next publication.
- **Recommendation**: Prove the authority and resource handoff with one real Crowd person and the hula cycle first, then reuse that narrow contract for the pair and dance without altering protected occupants or established frame ordering.
