# Temporary activities for existing people

## Intent

Existing eligible adults can join hula-hooping, paired frisbee and dancing, react to an approaching or honking cart, and either resume safely or return to ordinary behavior without changing identity or appearance. Deliver and verify the complete hula cycle before migrating frisbee and dancing.

## Scope

Use Crowd-owned people and its public claims and render commands. Activities own their props, place reservations and session policy. Preserve explicit selection, existing frame barriers, RNG streams, physical constraints and ownership rules. Replace specialized activity-only population lifetimes where their live behavior migrates; do not create a second collider or body for a borrowed person.

## Acceptance

A production-backed fixture and the real game demonstrate an ordinary person entering hula, dodging a charging and honking cart, resuming only after safety and resource checks, then finishing and walking away with the same handle and appearance. Paired frisbee handles interruption in held, flying and landed states, with both participants coordinated and no stranded disc or claims. Dancing follows the same interruption contract where physical exit is possible. Tests cover stale identities, resource replacement, competing claims, unload and disposal, collision and rendering authority, potty and vehicle constraints, and unchanged frame, audio and random-stream invariants.
