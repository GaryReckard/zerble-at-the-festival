## Architect's Position

### Priority Sequence

1. Define one authoritative player snapshot (position, quaternion, vector velocity, signed longitudinal speed, contact count, surface/water identity, engine effort and terminal state) and its publication barrier. `Zerble.position` currently aliases the render root, and main publishes movement to stats, map and sound before the late physical-world step (`src/zerble.js:71`; `src/main.js:1260-1274`; `src/main.js:1797`). Make the dynamic solve available before gameplay consumers; presentation interpolation must never become simulation input.
2. Add vehicle controllers as adapter-owned, generation-checked resources, with explicit create/read/control/remove methods and counts. Remove a controller before its chassis and clear it on disposal, following the existing joint/load ownership pattern (`src/physics/rapierAdapter.js:655-686`). Keep one world and one clock; apply wheel forces before the existing world step.
3. Reserve player terrain and nearby solids before admitting movement. Retain current tiles while replacements prepare, give the player priority before optional chairs, and include a bounded projected landing corridor. The clock already drops unavailable time instead of building catch-up debt (`src/physics/fixedStep.js:12-24`); use that contract rather than inventing a second scheduler. The existing capacities and queue limits are explicit resource contracts, so account for the new owner rather than silently extending the working set (`src/physics/liveWorld.js:27-38`; `src/physics/terrainResidency.js:112-135`).
4. Introduce shared physical support/water sampling without weakening pedestrian route admission. Resolve support from actual ray contact, including bridge deck versus stream bed, then publish grounded support identity to legacy consumers. Add lake depth through the same vertex source used by collision and rendering, not a physics-only offset.
5. Replace grounded player movement, legacy cart position correction and scalar-speed impact inference together. Then integrate surface forces, engine effort, incident damage, terminal water/damage, rider quaternion inheritance and safe boarding/recovery. Verify the isolated course and the full game under resets, stalls, owner unload and mode transitions.

### Structural Risks Identified

- **Two movement authorities:** `Zerble.update()` calls `groundOnSurface()` every frame and writes height/rotation, while main subsequently runs collision correction and the kinematic proxy (`src/zerble.js:1324`; `src/main.js:1795-1799`). A dynamic chassis must bypass both physical repositioning paths. Keep legacy person/scoring callbacks as consequence consumers, with impulses or events sent to the physical owner, not direct render-root writes.

- **Support identity is not physical contact:** `createSupportBinding()` requires every footprint sample to remain traversable and can reject an unsupported footprint, while its sampler deliberately extrapolates the last accepted plane after missing support (`src/landscape/support.js:136-162`; `src/landscape/support.js:187-195`). Do not use its traversability or fallback plane as the dynamic wheel ground. Keep missing geometry readiness separate from airborne contact loss; reacquire support on actual grounded contact rather than requiring a valid footprint during flight.

- **Rendered and resident support must share ownership:** landscape retains a bridge for movers by their selected support identity (`src/landscape/runtime.js:57-70`), while physics tiles separately pin water/district owners (`src/landscape/physicsTiles.js:5-24`). An airborne cart must retain collision support via the tile reservation even while it has no selected grounded deck. Release reservations only after replacement readiness or explicit owner teardown.

- **Current static proxies are not vehicle collision truth:** scenery proxies use terrain Y and a conservative cylinder derived from horizontal radius, without authored source height or rotations (`src/physics/staticResidency.js:40-52`; `src/physics/staticResidency.js:62-67`). Audit bridges, rails, tree trunks and large structures before making them player-solid. A trunk-radius obstacle can remain approximate, but invisible lake-edge rings and broad footprint cylinders cannot become accidental drivable or blocking surfaces. Preserve registry entries and NPC avoidance while filtering the player physics policy; lake ownership deliberately survives host-chunk unloading (`AGENTS.md:100-103`; `src/lakes.js:596-598`).

- **Water depth can diverge across render and collision consumers:** production terrain triangles use `terrainVertexHeight()` and a fixed 10m lattice (`src/worldgen/terrain.js:110-146`); native stream water admission is a separate branch (`src/worldgen/terrain.js:151`). Keep the bed in shared terrain truth and avoid importing scene/runtime water state upstream into deterministic worldgen. Bridges must be selected by actual collider contact, not nearest X/Z water query (`src/landscape/physicsTiles.js:12-17`; `src/landscape/physicsTiles.js:28-38`).

- **Terminal state currently excludes Cruisin:** `RunState.endRun()` rejects inactive runs, while `endFestivalRun()` always records a local festival result and conditionally posts a global final (`src/runState.js:155-159`; `src/main.js:2002-2034`). Introduce a shared session-ending owner or a mode-aware wrapper, with an exactly-once latch, and retain Festival-only scoring/reporting semantics. Damage and drowning must not silently fail in Cruisin or upload its results as Festival scores.

- **Full transform inheritance is required:** current seats transform through the cart quaternion only when a terrain sampler is present (`src/zerble.js:1077-1089`), so physical flat-world mode also needs the full-transform path. Rider orientation is yaw-only today (`src/crowd.js:1680-1691`; `src/crowd.js:2107-2108`); publish a vehicle-seat quaternion and a grounded/boarding eligibility contract rather than teaching Crowd about Rapier handles.

- **Capability artifacts currently contradict the proposed behavior:** the existing carts capability requires arcade integration and terrain-pinned Y (`openspec/specs/carts/spec.md`, “Arcade driving physics” and “The drivable world is infinite”). Add explicit delta modifications for changed requirements instead of declaring “Modified Capabilities: None” and calling those normative SHALL clauses historical. Current AGENTS.md permits stale traced implementation details to be corrected; it does not make contradictory active contracts harmless.

### Verdict

- **Verdict**: Proceed with mitigations
- **Key Concern**: The authoritative solve, world reservations and downstream gameplay publication must form one explicit ownership boundary.
- **Recommendation**: Use the existing Rapier vehicle controller and shared fixed-step world, but complete the ownership reversal as one coherent integration. Keep physical support, pedestrian reachability and mode-specific score reporting distinct. Make controller teardown, readiness stalls, bridge/bed selection, terminal deduplication and passenger orientation acceptance tests rather than follow-up assumptions.
