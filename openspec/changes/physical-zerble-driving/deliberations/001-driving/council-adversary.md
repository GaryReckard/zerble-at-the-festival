### Vulnerabilities Found

- **Mode-dependent terminal no-op or inappropriate leaderboard write**: RunState.begin is called only for Festival mode (src/main.js:1036-1037), and RunState.endRun refuses inactive state (src/runState.js:155-158). Existing endFestivalRun unconditionally records a Festival local result, emits Festival analytics, potentially submits a global result, and selects only ran-dry/marshal copy (src/main.js:2002-2033). A generic water/damage caller would either do nothing in Cruisin or require starting the wrong lifecycle. Severity: High. Add an idempotent mode-neutral vehicle-terminal guard, reuse the end screen with cause-specific copy, and invoke Festival lifecycle/reporting only in Festival mode. Closing/restarting must honor Cruisin terminal state too: current close handler checks RunState.over only (src/main.js:2040).

- **Lake barriers survive a partial removal**: Lakes add a sealed lake_edge collider ring (src/lakes.js:596-598), and static physics residency includes lake_edge among its proxy kinds (src/physics/staticResidency.js:6-10). Removing them only from legacy collision candidates still leaves fixed cylinders for the chassis or wheel rays. Severity: High. Explicitly distinguish logical pedestrian water admission from player physical solids, preserve lake entry ownership, and test shoreline entry against both resolver and Rapier paths.

- **Shared traversability is an unsafe water switch**: sampleTerrain marks submerged stream bed nontraversable (src/worldgen/terrain.js:150-151), while support bindings permit steep grades but continue to reject water and unsupported wheel footprints (src/landscape/support.js:130-143). Bridge selection rejects closed side edges and missing owners (src/landscape/support.js:82-110). Globally making this binding traversable would also change walker/placement contracts; leaving it as the player's movement gate would block creek entry and physical departure. Severity: High. Keep pedestrian/placement policy strict, let physical contact determine vehicle support, and treat support identity as compatibility/boarding metadata rather than an X/Z authorization gate during flight.

- **Reservation starvation can permanently freeze the player**: Terrain requests account for all committed and wanted owner tiles before accepting a replacement and reject when tile/queue limits are exceeded (src/physics/terrainResidency.js:103-120). Live limits are 64 tiles, 32 queued, MAX_CHAIRS+4 owners, and 256 bodies (src/physics/liveWorld.js:27-38). Merely adding a player claim is insufficient if distant active aftermath retains capacity. Severity: High. Reserve a priority player neighborhood plus replacement headroom, bound prediction, test saturation while props are displaced, and distinguish temporary readiness stalls from unrecoverable admission failure. A safe failed-closed gate needs a recoverable failure path rather than an endless frozen cart.

- **Wheel rays can mistake scenery for the road**: The static residency contract explicitly creates conservative ground-anchored cylinders with 2×radius height and does not mirror authored geometry (src/physics/staticResidency.js:54-59). As the cart becomes dynamic those are no longer only furniture barriers: unfiltered wheel casts can support the chassis on these invisible approximations. Severity: High. Use a deliberate wheel-support filter and appropriate chassis obstacle proxies; test grazing a tree, leaving a ledge beside a tent, and passing below a bridge. The current chassis is one low cuboid (src/physics/liveWorld.js:62), so upside-down roof penetration also requires an explicit collision-hull choice.

- **Resting loads are not crash damage**: The adapter exposes contact-force values in newtons for every step (src/physics/rapierAdapter.js:635-640), while fixed-clock configuration and dropped time are explicit (src/physics/fixedStep.js:3-24). Integrating force magnitudes directly into a damage meter counts gravity/support or depends on timestep. Existing live prop impacts derive horizontal approach from heading×speed and discard terrain/non-prop pairs (src/physics/liveWorld.js:217-236), so that path also cannot establish hard-landing severity. Severity: High. Capture incoming three-dimensional closing velocity, distinguish suspension support from chassis impact, and latch an incident until genuine separation/rearm. Tests must cover steady parking, persistent wall throttle, glancing hits, falling roof-first, and multiple collider contacts in one crash.

- **Physical time and terminal time can diverge**: canStep may drop all accumulated time when support is missing (src/physics/fixedStep.js:16-21). Driving water/impact timers from render dt while simulation is suspended would drown a cart without accepted motion, and resuming with stale controller wheel state can create artificial impulse damage. Severity: Medium. Advance physical consequences with accepted solver time, reset impact history on teleport/rescue/dropped-time transitions, and do not accrue terminal timers behind menus or after terminal state.

- **Full quaternion must reach dependent transforms**: Zerble's position aliases root.position (src/zerble.js:71), and current update recomputes world forward/nozzle and spins wheels from scalar speed (src/zerble.js:1328-1349). Rider rotation is yaw-only in normal matrix composition (src/crowd.js:1685-1690), even though seat location follows transformed targets (src/crowd.js:2099-2108). Severity: Medium. Separate authoritative simulation from presentation, avoid reading interpolated roots back into physics, apply the chassis quaternion to riders, and prevent ground-based boarding/exiting during flight/immersion.

### Verdict

- **Verdict**: Proceed with mitigations.
- **Key Concern**: Replacing the cart's motion owner exposes existing support, contact and mode assumptions that an isolated vehicle demo cannot exercise.
- **Recommendation**: Use built-in Rapier vehicle support, but make ownership, both-mode endings, wheel-query filtering, priority residency and incident damage explicit acceptance requirements before default live integration. Keep existing RNG draws, lake ownership, Three shim and synchronous iOS Sound.init unchanged; historical council/importmap text is superseded by current AGENTS.md and Vite. The actual audio unlock chain is verified in src/sound.js:284-351 and should not be rearranged.
