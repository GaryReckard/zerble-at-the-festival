## Profiler's Position

### Priority Sequence

1. Keep one 60 Hz fixed-step world and replace the existing kinematic cart with one dynamic chassis plus four rays. The current clock already caps accepted work at five steps and drops unavailable-support time instead of banking a catch-up burst (`src/physics/fixedStep.js:3-26`). Establish actual landings at that step before considering 120 Hz, since doubling the world rate doubles chair/person simulation as well as the cart.
2. Give the player a separately named support reservation within the existing terrain/static managers, prioritized before optional prop admission. Reuse their shared streaming deadline and maximum-creation pump, rather than building an independent collision world or enlarging the world load radius. Current live caps are 64 terrain tiles, 128 static proxies, 256 bodies and 512 colliders (`src/physics/liveWorld.js:27-38`), with two creations per pump (`src/physics/liveWorld.js:165-167`). Check admission arithmetic with the player resident and old/new tile overlap, not just a fresh empty course.
3. Prepare bounded road/stream/lake candidate sets outside the physics substep and sample those locally at actual wheel contacts. Preserve exact surface boundaries, including bridge versus bed, while avoiding repeated regional graph queries. `nearestRoad` walks every segment of `arterialsNear` and allocates a result (`src/worldgen/roads.js:341-360`); calling it separately for every wheel at every substep unnecessarily multiplies that work.
4. Add vehicle step timing, contact/event counts, player blocked duration and residency counters to the existing diagnostics. Capture parked, boosted travel, ridge launch/landing, creek crossing, lake submersion and repeated teleports under Low and Mid. Separate geometry preparation stalls from solver cost. Main already attributes world preparation (`src/main.js:1753-1758`); keep that attribution rather than burying it in a generic vehicle update.
5. Keep the visible cart, wheel meshes and existing materials. Add the damage meter in DOM/HUD and drive existing audio nodes through a small reusable envelope. Validate ordinary rendering has no physics-debug mesh, added light, new caster or post-process pass. Then compare render counters and CPU timings against the identical seed/path baseline, and record physical-device acceptance separately.

### Performance Risks Identified

| Risk | Type | Severity | Trigger Condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Doubling the shared solver to make suspension look smoother | SteadyState | High | The current world clock is 60 Hz (`src/physics/fixedStep.js:3`); a 120 Hz replacement also advances every admitted chair and person twice as often (`src/physics/liveWorld.js:210-214`). Keep 60 Hz initially and use render interpolation. |
| Support preparation starving or freezing player movement | Alloc | High | Player geometry joins the same bounded residency as props; requests count both committed and desired tiles (`src/physics/terrainResidency.js:103-120`). Prioritize the player request and expose blocked-time diagnostics, with old support retained until replacement is ready. |
| Per-wheel road classification repeatedly walking regional geometry | SteadyState | High | Four wheels, every physics step, enter `nearestRoad`'s arterial/segment loops (`src/worldgen/roads.js:341-360`). Retain a bounded nearby candidate cache and test actual width rather than blindly using its cosmetic onRoad threshold. |
| Larger high-speed or airborne lookahead causes tile thrash | Alloc | Medium | Existing prediction is capped at 0.5 seconds and 16 meters, and reuses a previous containing window (`src/physics/supportWindow.js:8-16`). Preserve bounded lookahead, cover each next-step swept chassis extent, and test max-speed falls without growing reservations to an entire ballistic trajectory. |
| Repeated scalar sampling becomes avoidable JavaScript allocation | SteadyState | Medium | Existing selected-deck lookup materializes arrays (`src/landscape/runtime.js:26`), and live steps already create an incoming Map (`src/physics/liveWorld.js:212-213`). Use reusable scratch values for new wheel/surface/engine/damage state and do not add per-step full-world snapshots. |
| Resting contacts flood the shared event queue | SteadyState | Medium | The adapter drains every force event into allocated event/vector records, with a bounded queue (`src/physics/rapierAdapter.js:163,635-647`). Use meaningful chassis force thresholds, contact-episode deduplication, bounded impact processing and overflow checks; parked contact must not accumulate damage. |
| Debug collision lines accidentally remain in normal rendering | Draws/Alloc | Medium | `debugRender()` copies Rapier vertex/color buffers (`src/physics/rapierAdapter.js:650-653`). Keep it opt-in in the proof surface, not a per-frame prerequisite for vehicle state or damage. |
| A second visible lake bottom or splash system expands scope and GPU cost | Tris/Alloc | Medium | The plan promises shared physical/visual bed truth (`../../design.md`, Decisions). Prefer changing existing terrain vertices and retaining existing water meshes; measure any additional visual geometry explicitly rather than assuming physics is draw-free. |

### Budget Estimate

- **Draw delta:** Target +0 ordinary-game 3D draws by reusing Zerble's existing model/wheels, existing water mesh, and DOM damage HUD. This is a design target, not a measured result. No new lights, casters or screen passes are authorized by the briefing. The Low 80-draw guide remains the tightest nominal envelope, but current AGENTS.md says HUD guides do not establish device performance.
- **Triangle delta:** Target +0 rendered triangles if lake depth modifies existing terrain vertices rather than adding a second bottom. Collision mesh triangles are separately bounded and are not GPU-rendered triangles. At the current 20 m tile and 10 m terrain grid, each tile has four cells/eight collision triangles, making the existing 64-tile terrain cap 512 triangles before bridge slabs (`src/physics/terrainResidency.js:33,158-164`; `src/physics/terrainProxy.js:30-33`; `src/worldgen/terrain.js:14`). Do not add them to renderer.info totals.
- **Solver/query delta:** One dynamic body replaces one kinematic body, with four wheel rays per step. At 60 Hz this is 240 wheel rays per accepted simulation second and at most 20 per rendered update under the existing five-step cap (`src/physics/fixedStep.js:3,15`). This is an operation count, not a latency prediction; broad-phase occupancy, contacts and CCD remain relevant.
- **Cost type:** Both allocation/preparation stalls and steady-state CPU simulation, with little necessary GPU delta. Existing admission capacities are storage limits, explicitly not measured device budgets (`src/physics/rapierAdapter.js:164-166`).
- **Low/mid-tier verdict:** At risk until measured, but a bounded single-chassis design is practical. Verify CPU preparation and solver costs separately and retain existing shadow/material-tier behavior; no proactive geometry or render-pipeline overhaul is justified by this feature.

### Verdict

- **Verdict:** Proceed with mitigations.
- **Key Concern:** A dynamic player makes collision residency continuously mandatory, so optional-prop admission and expensive repeated surface queries must not control the player's frame delivery.
- **Recommendation:** Preserve the shared 60 Hz clock and world deadline, prioritize a bounded player support owner, reuse surface candidates and scratch state, keep normal 3D render deltas at zero, and measure Low/Mid travel and landing before declaring device performance acceptable. The proposed feature is chiefly a CPU ownership/query problem, not a draw-call problem.
