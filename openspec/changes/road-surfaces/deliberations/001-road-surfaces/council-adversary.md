## Adversary's Position

TL/DR: Proceed with mitigations, but revise the unconditional nine-metre paving rule before implementation. A production-worldgen probe already finds a major approach where widening adds a water intersection, and the existing nearest-centerline query cannot safely represent clearance against roads of different widths.

### Priority Sequence

1. Freeze route-point fixtures and the existing point-query fields, then settle shoreline eligibility for paving. The draft promises unchanged routes and wider major approaches, but current water rejection checks only sampled centerlines (`src/worldgen/roads.js:123`, `src/worldgen/roads.js:196`; `design.md:13`, `design.md:29`).
2. Define separate centerline-facing and footprint-clearance queries before migrating placement. Keep the generous existing no-build corridor unchanged, and test clearance against every relevant edge with that edge's width (`src/worldgen/roads.js:303`, `src/worldgen/roads.js:359`; `src/worldgen/index.js:65`).
3. Characterize planner RNG consumption before widening placement gates, including rejected food-court candidates. Preserving the textual order of RNG calls does not preserve their execution count (`src/worldgen/festival.js:554`, `src/worldgen/festival.js:1034`).
4. Exercise complete production paths for detailed geometry and copied distant snapshots, including terrain clipping, capacity overflow and two unload/revisit cycles. Capture Low/Mid Noon/Midnight and the ready-click game boot already required by `tasks.md:16`.

### Vulnerabilities Found

- **Unconditional paving adds water intersections**: The generator accepts roads using water probes at intervals up to 18 m along the centerline, with no ribbon-width expansion (`src/worldgen/roads.js:123`). A read-only Node probe against the current source, with seed `3248888364`, found the major heart `(-1,-6)` at `(-117,-1129)` connected to the minor heart `(-1,-5)` at `(-84,-935)`. On segment `(-107,-1097) → (-126,-1061)`, at `t=36/41`, the left edge at offset `4.5` lands at `(-127.66265821707967,-1067.4906576904507)` and `lakeAt` returns true, while the center and same-side offset `3.5` return false. This was obtained by enumerating major-incident `arterial(a,b)` paths and checking both edge offsets at one-metre segment intervals. It proves that preserving routes and widening every major approach is insufficient for shoreline safety. Add a deterministic full-footprint paving eligibility test with a whole-edge dirt fallback, or explicitly specify another shoreline treatment; do not silently move the centerline. Characterize inherited dirt-road shoreline defects separately because centerline sampling does not establish their absence either. Severity: **High**.

- **Nearest centerline is not nearest road boundary**: `nearestRoad` chooses minimum centerline distance, and `arterialsNear` currently retains only polylines, discarding edge identity (`src/worldgen/roads.js:291`, `src/worldgen/roads.js:303`). Adding the winning road's width to this return value does not make placement safe. For example, a point 5 m from a dirt centerline and 5.5 m from a paved centerline passes the dirt half-width plus 1 m shoulder, yet touches the paved half-width plus shoulder. This is a mathematical counterexample to using the winning centerline for variable-width clearance, rather than a claim that this coordinate was found in the game. Retain centerline distance/direction for facing, but evaluate shape clearance per road, as the existing all-segment helper already does structurally (`src/worldgen/roads.js:359`). Update its per-edge expansion, food-court checks and woodland checks together (`src/worldgen/festival.js:1048`; `src/woodlandCamping.js:41`). Also migrate the layout linter: its booth and truck checks still hard-code the old ribbon width, so a green linter could miss newly occupied pavement (`src/worldgen/lint.js:468`, `src/worldgen/lint.js:589`). Severity: **High**.

- **Clearance changes can shift planner RNG without moving a single call**: `_computePlan` owns one `SALT.poiLayout` stream (`src/worldgen/festival.js:957`). `nudgeOff` conditionally consumes a draw only when its initial candidate is blocked, and food-court search calls it repeatedly before width rejection and subsequent yaw selection (`src/worldgen/festival.js:554`, `src/worldgen/festival.js:1045`, `src/worldgen/festival.js:1050`). Wider offsets or rejection gates can therefore change later draws and unrelated descriptors, contrary to the migration promise to avoid incidental RNG shifts (`design.md:29`). Preserve the original candidate/draw schedule and perform added width repair using a fresh independent salt, or explicitly characterize and authorize the full affected planner migration. A query-order test alone cannot detect this regression; compare old and new downstream descriptors and original route fixtures. Severity: **High**.

- **A new helper can regress two independent depth fixes**: The detailed road currently requires lazy shared material construction, `depthWrite:true`, polygon offset, a 0.06 m lift and terrain-triangle clipping (`src/chunks.js:887`, `src/chunks.js:899`, `src/chunks.js:866`, `src/chunks.js:1010`). `git blame -L 887,910 src/chunks.js` attributes the lazy/shared rationale to Gary's `db4e0ef5` and the explicit depth-write correction to Gary's `5e0c6885`. The later uncommitted crease correction establishes why vertex heights alone cannot protect triangle interiors (`verification/people/stage-audience.md:49`; `src/worldgen/terrain.js:154`). Preserve both, generate UVs from the final clipped positions, and retain shared material identity through unload. The plan already calls for these mitigations, but they must become explicit regression assertions when extracting the helper. Severity: **High**.

- **Distant color metadata can be discarded or multiplied by the old brown tint**: The current pipeline strips road records to flat arrays in `expandFarInstances`, uses one fixed half-width in `_writeRoads`, and resets the shared material to brown on every nightness refresh (`src/farField.js:309`, `src/farField.js:910`, `src/farField.js:1026`). Carry surface/width through all three stages, use a neutral material multiplier for per-vertex surface colors, and populate colors for the vertices produced by terrain clipping, not merely the original vertices. Preserve whole-road overflow rejection after clipping and dirty every changed attribute (`src/farField.js:979`, `src/farField.js:991`). Otherwise Noon/Midnight can produce differently tinted asphalt or missing/corrupted road sections after a distant snapshot rewrite. Severity: **Medium**.

- **Metadata can invalidate the frozen golden even with identical routes**: `queryPoint` currently exposes the existing road fields but no surface/width (`src/worldgen/index.js:65`), while the golden hashes `JSON.stringify` of the entire tuple (`src/worldgen/selftest.js:100`). Appending metadata therefore changes the golden by schema alone, and replacing the expected hash would obscure whether old fields drifted. Preserve an explicit projection of the original tuple for the frozen existence golden and add a separate metadata fixture if the public tuple expands, or keep new metadata in dedicated road descriptors. Retain `onRoad` and the broader no-build corridor semantics independently of visual width (`src/worldgen/roads.js:320`; `design.md:17`). Severity: **Medium**.

### Verdict

- **Verdict**: Proceed with mitigations
- **Key Concern**: The proposed major-incident nine-metre rule demonstrably adds a shoreline intersection on an unchanged route.
- **Recommendation**: Resolve deterministic shoreline eligibility, separate footprint clearance from centerline facing, and protect planner draw execution before rendering work. The draft already preserves Safari's module rules, Low-tier fallback, synchronous audio and frame ownership; retain those boundaries without incidental refactoring (`src/threeShim.js:12`, `src/threeShim.js:31`; `src/sound.js:271`; `src/main.js:973`). This is a source-and-probe deliberation, not browser acceptance evidence.
