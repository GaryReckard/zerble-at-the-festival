# Physical festival source baseline, October 30, 2026

This is the Task 1.1 (Baseline and compatibility) source ledger for the physical-festival plan. The inspected checkout is `codex/physical-festival` at `17102a1`. The parent task recorded that revision as the clean post-hoop baseline and recorded an initial `npm run check` pass before subsequent edits. At inspection time, the shared worktree had uncommitted edits to `package.json`, `package-lock.json`, `vite.config.mjs`, `openspec/changes/physical-festival/session-log.md`, and new physics files and harnesses. The existing production files cited below had no diff from `17102a1`, so this report treats their current contents as the baseline. The source ledger is not a runtime, browser, phone, visual, or performance acceptance report.

## Verified source ledger

| Area | Current source | Baseline fact and integration implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chair rendering | [`batchCampChairs`](../../src/models/campsite.js#L1052) | It removes each chair group's child meshes and emits geometry/material-keyed `InstancedMesh` batches. The chair groups remain for seat metadata, but no per-chair instance-slot binding is retained. Physical chair motion therefore needs an explicit mapping from logical chair/parts to render slots and must update instance transforms and bounds. |
| Chair recipes and seats | [`buildCampsite`](../../src/models/campsite.js#L1305), [`registerCampsite`](../../src/campRegistry.js#L89) | Chair and loveseat seats are independent slots; a loveseat's two claims share one furniture collider. Registration stores transformed seat positions and approach points on the campsite entry. A physical transfer must preserve both seat identities and the shared collider relationship. |
| Cooler ownership/collider | [`buildCampsite`](../../src/models/campsite.js#L1301), [`registerCampsite`](../../src/campRegistry.js#L107) | The camp kitchen and cooler are both children of the canopy group. The cooler gets a radius-0.42 registry collider at a transformed offset, but no independent cooler descriptor/group is returned. Moving that current parent would move the canopy with it; the collider is a broad proxy, not a movable cooler body. |
| Drink population and visual | [`Crowd` drink mesh creation](../../src/crowd.js#L332), [`spawn` drink-slot allocation](../../src/crowd.js#L721), [`drink transform publication](../../src/crowd.js#L1817) | Drinks use one shared geometry and an instanced mesh, with per-NPC slot/color state. The drink is hidden when photography, camp instruments, activity arm poses, or hula take precedence. Hiding is a render choice, not an explicit transfer/drop state, so cup ownership must avoid drawing a second held or world cup. |
| Activity and hand authority | [`claimActivity` and pose methods](../../src/crowd.js#L2315), [`readActivityHand`](../../src/crowd.js#L2351), [`readActivityPresentation`](../../src/crowd.js#L2360) | Activity writes and reads require the live activity claim; hand and presentation reads also reject hidden/party rigs. Use these public claim-checked capabilities for prop attachment and reactions rather than reaching into private NPC records or treating a render slot as identity. |
| Completed hoop compatibility | [`PairSession` acquisition](../../src/people/hoop/pairSession.js#L22), [`workshop` hand-contact checks](../../src/people/hoop/workshop.js#L140) | The completed hoop partner activity acquires two live activity claims before playing, and the workshop verifies contact against the public published arm presentation. Physics-side hand attachments must preserve those claims and must not create a competing arm/hand publisher. |
| Production contact barrier | [`main.js` people barriers](../../src/main.js#L1355), [`world and star ordering](../../src/main.js#L1669), [`legacy contact pass](../../src/main.js#L1684) | Adults and other scheduled people work run before world streaming; star pickup updates before the legacy contact pass, so same-frame star mode gates that pass. The legacy pass is also gated by invulnerability. Physical stepping must have independent damage eligibility and ghost filtering, and it must route each candidate to one response owner. |
| Terrain surface | [`sampleTerrain`](../../src/worldgen/terrain.js#L125) | Ground height and gradient follow the rendered grid's diagonal: the cell splits from `(x+step,z)` to `(x,z+step)`, and sampling selects one plane by `u+v`. Physics ground must use this exact triangle split, including terrain-disabled flat ground, rather than bilinear interpolation. |
| Canonical generation clearance | [`festival.js` plan occupancy](../../src/worldgen/festival.js#L955), [`chunk campsite placement](../../src/chunks.js#L2729), [`registry clearance](../../src/campRegistry.js#L126), [`built-world lint](../../src/worldgen/lint.js#L436) | Festival descriptors pack against their deterministic planned `placed` shapes. Chunk campsite placement also checks roads/lakes and either existing registry solids or camp-neighborhood clearance before registering the built camp; the worldgen lint separately checks built obstacles against authoritative dancefloor geometry. Moving props must update live avoidance/collision without feeding accident poses back into the canonical generator or its planned/seeded clearance result. |
| Occupied-seat origin ownership | [`seat slots are registered under chunk ownership](../../src/campRegistry.js#L104), [`slot reservation validity](../../src/people/campResources.js#L7), [`chunk teardown](../../src/chunks.js#L579) | Camp seat tokens are tied to the live activity claim, exact registry entry, and original slot object. Chunk teardown removes all entries indexed by `chunkKey`; it does not transfer an occupied slot or furniture automatically. Adoption therefore needs a synchronous ownership transfer/rollback that keeps the claim valid and prevents old-owner teardown from deleting the adopted resource. |

## Integration schedule and compatibility consequence

The design's [production frame contract](../../openspec/changes/physical-festival/design.md#L78) is still a proposed schedule, not code already present. Its source compatibility points are the ordinary people barriers at `main.js:1355-1418`, streaming at `main.js:1669-1674`, same-frame star activation at `main.js:1676-1682`, and the legacy contact gate at `main.js:1684-1709`.

| Order | Existing authority or proposed work | Constraint to verify during implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accepted gameplay tick | Existing accepted delta and gameplay gates feed the frame. | Physics must consume that accepted delta and pause with gameplay; do not add a second RAF or wall clock. |
| Ordinary cart and people | Current vehicle, spatial and adult barriers run before collection and world work. | Steering can read the last solved prop state, but each people scheduler must still run once. |
| Collection and world work | Collection/scoring and `updateWorld` occur before star/contact handling. | Keep bounded streaming and registry publication ahead of physical candidate construction. |
| Same-frame star state | `StarPower.update` precedes the existing collision gate. | Apply explicit cart ghost filtering to physics in the same frame; damage invulnerability must not stop simulation. |
| Late contact barrier | Existing `resolveCollision` is the current legacy response point and runs only when damage-eligible and not star-powered. | Route physical candidates away from legacy collision response while leaving nonphysical candidates there; prove one response per contact. |
| Solver and presentation, proposed | The design proposes stepping after same-frame activation, then publishing affected poses for rendering. | Restore authoritative solved poses for queries, publish interpolated poses only for display, and use a pose-only person publication that does not advance activity time. The frame trace must prove ordering and one-presentation-frame steering latency before acceptance. |
