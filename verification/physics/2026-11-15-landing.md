# Landing forgiveness, November 15, 2026

## Behavior

Small hops and soft touchdowns do not damage the cart. Both wheel touchdowns and upward-facing terrain/deck chassis impacts require at least 0.5 seconds airborne and more than 8 m/s normal impact speed. Serious landings retain quadratic damage with the existing 25-point cap. Trees, cliff faces and other obstacle impacts retain their existing damage rules, including during flight.

The shared selector is [vehicleImpact.js](../../src/physics/vehicleImpact.js). [playerVehicle.js](../../src/physics/playerVehicle.js) measures wheel touchdown against contact normals, preserving completed airtime for 0.25 seconds after touchdown so a subsequent chassis contact uses the same landing policy. [vehicleCondition.js](../../src/vehicleCondition.js) keeps harmless contacts out of the shared cooldown. Production and the driving sandbox both call the selector.

`node bin/test-player-driving-live` executes the real Rapier live-world owner and checks complete body/collider/controller teardown. Vertical drops begin above settled flat terrain, then run three seconds at each of 30, 60 and 120 Hz presentation while physics remains at 60 Hz.
