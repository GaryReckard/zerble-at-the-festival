# October 9 guided performance playtests

Gary ran the fixed-seed (`3948869160`) guided scenarios on a Mac M4 Pro in Chrome and an iPhone in Safari. The source JSON files are local and ignored by Git under `.claude/captures/`; run `bin/report-perf-playtest .claude/captures/<filename>` to reproduce a summary. These measurements describe these two devices and runs, not a population FPS estimate.

| Scenario | Local capture | Quality path | Comparable phase result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mac, High, drive | `device-mv18we55-i4xp32.json` | Baseline DPR 2 and fancy bubbles stepped to DPR 1.5 and cheap bubbles within 20 s. | 90 s driving, about 2,212 m travelled; three frames over 50 ms, including 160.6 ms render work near `(1043,-31)`. |
| iPhone, Low, drive | `device-mv190ygp-05705n.json` | Cheap bubbles were present at the first sample; DPR stepped from 1.3 to 0.6 within 11 s. | 91 s driving, about 2,199 m travelled; no frames over 50 ms in the drive phase. |
| Mac, High, Trip | `device-mv195625-vjh7m1.json` | Settled at DPR 1.5 and cheap bubbles; the scenario then locked that quality for comparison. | Baseline, active, peak, and after held near 60 reported FPS; fade-in included 216.8 ms render work. |
| iPhone, Low, Trip rerun | `device-mv19asuu-6d3e08.json` | Settled at DPR 0.6 and cheap bubbles; the scenario then locked that quality for comparison. | Baseline through after held 58–59 median reported FPS; no post-settling frame over 50 ms. |

The first iPhone Trip (`device-mv1989aq-0sck1u.json`) was interrupted by screen lock and is excluded. The clean rerun has zero visibility interruptions and zero captured errors. Both drives completed and are useful for driving analysis, but their `parked-after` phases moved about 250 m on Mac and 26 m on iPhone, so those phases are not valid stationary recovery controls. A subsequent capture-workflow fix pauses driving and clears speed for future parked phases; it does not change these older reports.

The iPhone's post-settling Trip frame-gap p95 stayed around 37–39 ms even at DPR 0.6, while the worst measured frame work in each comparison phase was 9–10 ms. The drive showed similarly elevated frame-gap p95 (36–38 ms) after settling with much lower render work. This is evidence that the governor's wall-time trigger needs a controlled check against browser scheduling and presentation cadence; it does **not** establish that the GPU can sustain the full visual tier. The governor uses raw wall-clock frame gaps and can downgrade on p95 over 33 ms or sustained average over 22 ms ([adaptiveQuality.js](../../../src/adaptiveQuality.js)); Low intentionally starts with cheap bubbles ([adaptiveQuality.js](../../../src/adaptiveQuality.js)). The Trip scenario freezes the quality rung reached after settling ([debug.js](../../../src/debug.js)), so its stable comparison phases do not measure initial full quality.
