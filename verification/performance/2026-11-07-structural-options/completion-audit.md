## Verification and convergence

The two rendering raw files contain six views × two mechanisms × eight ordered
blocks × 24 samples, or 2,304 measured renders per tier. The independent factual
audit recalculated every median row rather than relying on the summary generator,
and found zero qualifying nomination views for either mechanism on either tier.
The fixed summary also regenerated byte-identically:
[regeneration receipt](render-regeneration-check.json).

Documentation-only final checks inspect local links, generated README status,
source preservation, diagnostic syntax and whitespace; their exact result is
recorded in [documentation checks](documentation-checks.json). Production full
checks and a new ordinary acceptance campaign are not substituted for the
mechanism-specific probes or required for this negative research decision.
The copied diagnostic build completed Vite build and actual ready-title hardware
integration in both tested tiers, without modifying the production checkout.

The final synthesis requires only evidence handoff and truthful closure. Those
are represented here and in the completed investigation checklist. Physical
iPad/Auto acceptance remains an independent pre-existing limitation, and the
adult eligibility/upload census remains a possible future research entry point.
Neither is silently marked passed or turned into an obligation to continue until
some candidate wins.
