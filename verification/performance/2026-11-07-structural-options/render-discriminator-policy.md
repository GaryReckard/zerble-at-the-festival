# Rendering discriminator, declared before capture

This is a finite feasibility experiment after the exact-support and registry
prototypes did not justify selection. No production implementation is authorized
by the experiment, and a lower drawing-request count alone cannot select a rewrite.

## Mechanisms and baseline

The baseline is the frozen current application identified by
[source-manifest.json](source-manifest.json), including the November 6 terrain
lattice work. The disposable build exposes the unchanged existing
`createChildPresentation` factory only on a local diagnostic URL. Its complete
source/build identity and sole bootstrap change are recorded in
[render-build-provenance.json](render-build-provenance.json). Ordinary source files
are not modified.

The narrow matrix-only comparison selects direct opaque booth meshes from the
actual `userData.evening.vendor` booth family. Those booth bodies are already
merged (`src/models/tent.js:207`), so a small opportunity is an expected legitimate
negative result, not permission to freeze unrelated animated hierarchies.
Three r160 `Object3D.updateMatrixWorld` propagates a forced parent update through
children even when a child disables `matrixWorldAutoUpdate`; local composition
and world traversal must not be conflated (`node_modules/three/src/core/Object3D.js:572-608`).

The separate submission comparison groups eligible shopkeeper source parts into
80-metre world cells and invokes the real child-presentation factory on those
records. It preserves all source rigs and simulation. It tests bounded draw
publication, not compact pose storage, offscreen simulation removal or a unified
NPC framework. Incompatible or changed render state must retain the ordinary
path, with the actual skip/fallback inventory recorded.

## Required evidence

1. The actual ready-title gesture, hardware renderer, focus and rendering target
   dimensions must pass. The accepted supervisor owns the browser, worker,
   deadlines and exact process teardown.
2. Each baseline/candidate pair holds the scene pose and camera fixed. Every
   original world matrix, camera state and registry fingerprint must agree;
   disposing adapters must restore layers and leave shared resources owned by
   their original systems.
3. Report eligibility, source nodes, batch objects and actual submitted draws
   and triangles. The initial unculled topology inventory is not saved draws.
4. Preserve raw render-work and RAF wall samples in alternating ABBA blocks,
   shader warmup and screenshot pairs. A frozen scene is attribution, never an
   accepted ordinary-route or device-performance result.
5. Exercise third-person, first-person and top cameras at Noon and Midnight.
   Low and High are separate captures. Check camera-dependent shadow/triangle
   amplification and fallback rather than relying on a single favorable view.

## Decision rule

A correctness or visible rendering mismatch rejects the mechanism as written.
An improvement in submission count accompanied by neutral/adverse render work,
substantial triangle amplification or unstable pair direction does not nominate
a major rewrite. A matrix-only saving also does not prove removal of hierarchy
cost or justify a compact-pose rewrite.

A candidate may advance to an ordinary live-game pilot only if repeated paired
blocks show a useful net reduction, the protected views show no material new
cost, and the audited eligible family is large enough to support the proposed
scope. A live pilot can nominate a finalist but final performance acceptance
still requires fresh calibrated parked/driving comparisons, as specified by the
[first council](../../../openspec/changes/structural-performance-redesign/deliberations/001-options/results.md).
No percentage or FPS improvement is promised, and the prior terrain experiment's
thresholds are not reused.

For this investigation's pre-capture nomination filter, useful means at least
1 ms less median render work in two protected views, with the paired direction
preserved in both baseline-first and candidate-first block groups, and no view
showing more than 0.5 ms worse median work. One millisecond is an engineering
investment threshold, about six percent of a 60 Hz frame allowance, not a
statistical detection limit or a claimed frame-rate increase. Both timing and
triangle/draw outcomes remain visible even if the filter fails. This filter is
declared before either rendering capture and is not the lab's ordinary acceptance
policy; it only prevents selecting a major rewrite for a tiny subsystem saving.

If this probe does not justify advancement, reassess the remaining inventory
against actual source/profile evidence and obtain an adversarial selection audit.
The user explicitly permits stopping with no worthwhile structural rewrite;
exhaustive production planning is conditional on selecting one, not a requirement
to manufacture a winner after a fixed number of experiments.
