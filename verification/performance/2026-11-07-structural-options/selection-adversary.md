# Adversarial selection audit

TL/DR: A worthwhile shopkeeper presentation result can justify planning that bounded representation change, but it cannot by itself justify compact poses or a generic rewrite of every secondary person. If it fails, the defensible conclusion is that the tested mechanisms have not earned a rewrite; claiming that no worthwhile structural path remains would leave child solving, construction tails and retained rig transforms unresolved. Close that gap with one finite residual-cost triage, not seven new prototypes.

## What would justify investing in a presentation plan

1. **Identify the work actually removed.** The existing presenter retains every original rig, disables ordinary mesh layers, and copies current world matrices into instances, so reusing it for shopkeepers can reduce submission but cannot demonstrate removal of source hierarchy transforms. Report family-specific eligible objects, ordinary submissions and replacement buckets, source traversal, bounds/culling and matrix-upload work, plus caster behavior. Global composer attribution and unculled topology counts cannot supply this attribution (`src/people/childPresentation.js:23-25`, `:33-55`, `:69-106`; `candidate-inventory.md:10-20`; `openspec/changes/structural-performance-redesign/deliberations/001-options/results.md:83-85`).
2. **Show a net, representative opportunity.** An isolated same-content probe must include presentation synchronization, camera/shadow work, allocation/admission and teardown, and preserve unfavorable tiers/order effects. A substantial family-specific benefit can nominate a timing candidate, while a short ordinary comparison can decide whether investing in an exhaustive implementation plan is sensible. Before that comparison, record its meaningful absolute benefit and protected regression rules. Do not substitute fewer draws or a large percentage of a tiny cost for that decision (`deliberations/001-options/results.md:25`, `:40-44`, `:55-57`, relative to the active change; `registry-probe-report.md:39-61`).
3. **Bound the generalization.** Plan an eligibility contract and fallback for other secondary people rather than declaring all rigs supported: the actual helper rejects transparent, skinned, instanced, custom-depth and morph-bearing meshes, and falls back on changed state/layers or shear. Its current behavior is a useful oracle, not proof that every new family qualifies. Shopkeepers themselves are explicitly excluded from the tent's static merge because they are animated (`src/people/childPresentation.js:35-41`, `:85-101`; `src/models/tent.js:198-205`).

A compact-pose plan needs separate mechanism-specific evidence that retained hierarchy evaluation/copying is materially expensive and that direct poses can replace that cost. Successful rig-retaining shopkeeper batching does not supply it. Existing child matrix, transformed-parent, camera, shadow and disposal tests provide a bounded oracle for a later compact-pose discriminator (`render-actor-source-map.md:20-24`; `src/people/childPresentation.js:98-105`; active-change `deliberations/001-options/results.md:57`).

There are two gates, and neither should be renamed to hide missing evidence. A feasible isolated result can justify a **conditional plan to prove a candidate**, but the existing deliberation reserves a **selected production recommendation** for equivalence, lifecycle/budget checks and meaningful ordinary delivered-frame evidence. Physical Safari, Auto, long-run retention and the full camera/time/tier matrix remain future production acceptance work; they are not prerequisites for writing a clearly conditional experiment/integration plan (`openspec/changes/structural-performance-redesign/deliberations/001-options/results.md:42`, `:54-59`, `:66-77`).

## Finite decision if the render probe fails

The logical gap is between “support, registry and this presentation mechanism failed” and “none of the inventoried structural mechanisms is worthwhile.” The inventory expressly contains unmeasured solving, compact-pose and construction candidates, while the council makes their promotion conditional on mechanism-specific evidence (`candidate-inventory.md:29-32`; active-change `deliberations/001-options/results.md:57`, `:97`).

The minimum corrective investigation I recommend is **one fixed residual-cost triage**, initially using the saved exact-build profiles and stage data: classify the largest remaining actionable costs into child solver/reprojection, retained rig transforms/presentation, ordinary construction tails, or already protected/unexplained work. If saved data cannot distinguish the first three, permit one bounded diagnostic route with only the missing per-frame counters/stage links; do not build alternative mechanisms or repeatedly change the route. This follows the existing requirement for actual passes, stage attribution and mechanism-specific evidence (`render-actor-source-map.md:16-18`, `:22-30`; `streaming-source-map.md:140-148`; active-change `deliberations/001-options/results.md:57`).

Freeze the triage roster and meaningful-benefit rule before reading its result. Promote **at most one** remaining mechanism only if the removable work, eligible population and protected behavior support a credible absolute benefit exceeding that rule; otherwise stop and state “no inventoried candidate earned restructuring from this investigation.” An unexplained residual is a measurement limit, not proof of universal impossibility, and does not require an endless search. This finite stopping rule is this audit's recommendation, not an existing repository policy; the underlying council explicitly permits no rewrite and rejects selecting from overlapping samples or work counts alone (`openspec/changes/structural-performance-redesign/deliberations/001-options/results.md:42-44`, `:57-59`, `:83-86`).
