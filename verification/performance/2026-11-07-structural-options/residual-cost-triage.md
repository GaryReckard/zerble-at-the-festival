# Finite residual-cost triage

TL/DR: The remaining inventory supplies plausible mechanisms but no additional candidate that presently earns an exhaustive production redesign plan. Adult spatial draw publication is the strongest remaining lead, while child solving and retained rig transforms lack isolated attribution. If the bounded shopkeeper probe fails, it is honest to stop with “no inventoried candidate earned restructuring from this investigation”; it would be inaccurate to say every structural mechanism was disproved. No further runtime discriminator is compulsory to make that scoped decision.

This is a retrospective offline review of already inspected evidence, not a preregistered selection policy. No benchmark, test, build, browser or production edit was performed. Extracted exact rows, profile hashes and captured population ranges are saved in `residual-cost-triage.json`.

## Verified evidence shape

All three actual `*-all-functions.json` files have top-level fields `scope`, `totalSampledMs`, `self`, `inclusive` and `reachabilityCallers`; each row has `function`, `file`, `line` and `ms`. There is no `functions` collection or per-row `selfMs`/`totalMs`. Their sampled totals are 59,840.666 ms for High route, 60,490.879 ms for Low route and 29,517.608 ms for High parked. They explicitly describe sampling attribution rather than elapsed loop timers. Inclusive rows overlap, and absent rows below mean “no matching sampled row,” not a zero-duration call (`high-route-all-functions.json`, `low-route-all-functions.json`, `high-parked-all-functions.json`; exact selections and SHA-256 hashes in `residual-cost-triage.json:profiles`).

The associated saved `capture.json` files show 50 children in every capture, 215–354 adults in High route, 119–180 in Low route and 305–307 in High parked. Their sample fields include populations, registry size, draw/triangle counts and route state, but no solver-pass counts, actor visibility/caster census or per-frame ordinary-builder stage timings. These captures are located through the saved `state.json:profiles[*].directory`; the exact paths and inspected field names are retained in `residual-cost-triage.json:profiles.*.capture`. Whole-frame timings therefore cannot be assigned to the candidate mechanisms from these exports alone.

## Finite selection judgment

None of the five remaining mechanisms currently supplies both a mechanism-specific materially removable workload and evidence that the replacement can improve it without moving protected behavior. This does not mean all are infeasible: the exact missing discriminator is listed in each row, and adult spatial publication has the strongest source-grounded reason to revisit. The existing council explicitly conditions child solving, compact poses and construction on mechanism-specific evidence and permits no rewrite, rather than requiring a prototype of every plausible idea (`openspec/changes/structural-performance-redesign/deliberations/001-options/results.md:42-44`, `:57-59`, `:97`; the source/profile evidence above).

Accordingly, **no additional diagnostic run is required before an honest scoped no-rewrite verdict** if the final shopkeeper submission experiment fails. The complete statement should be: “The tested support and registry mechanisms did not earn advancement; the bounded rendering result also failed; the remaining inventory was reviewed offline and lacked sufficient mechanism-specific evidence to justify another redesign experiment.” Do not replace that with “rendering/children/streaming have no optimization opportunity.” This finite triage resolves the scope gap identified in `selection-adversary.md` without turning the task into universal proof that every possible rewrite fails.

If the root chooses to spend one final discriminator despite that stopping judgment, choose **the adult visibility/caster/upload census only**, because it addresses the strongest remaining concrete representation issue. Do not simultaneously instrument five candidates or reinterpret the already viewed profiles as a new preregistered threshold. Such a census could justify a later bounded spatial-publication experiment, but would still not select a production rewrite; ordinary equal-content comparison and lifecycle/visual/device acceptance remain separate future gates (`src/crowd.js:511-522`; active-change `deliberations/001-options/results.md:54-59`).
