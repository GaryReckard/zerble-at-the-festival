# Built-in image generation prompts

The built-in `image_gen` tool was used in edit mode with `transparent_background: true`. Each original local WebP was inspected before use. These are the exact prompt texts. Original PNG generations remain in Codex's generated-images directory; project-bound WebP exports are listed in [assets.json](assets.json).

## Sky

Reference/edit target: `assets/title-screen/layer-sky.webp`.

> Use case: precise-object-edit / outpainting. Edit target: the supplied transparent sunset SKY layer from a layered pixel-painted game title screen. Produce one extended SKY layer only, on a 16:9 transparent canvas, ideally 2560x1440. Expand the canvas on ALL four sides so the existing image occupies exactly the centered two-thirds of the output width and two-thirds of the output height. Keep the source image composition, sun position, scale relative to its original area, brushwork, pixel texture and colors unchanged inside that central rectangle. Outpaint matching indigo/purple evening sky and pink/orange/gold cloud formations into the new margins. Extend the dark purple upper sky enough to reach the new top edge and the cloud field continuously to both side edges; carry the warm lower horizon colors to the bottom. This is the farthest background layer, so fill the expanded sky field rather than drawing a vignette with empty outer margins. Preserve any original alpha cutouts in the original central source region. No mountains, lake, festival tents, structures, people, vehicles, logos, words or bubbles. Do not add a second sun. Return only this individual registered game background layer, not a contact sheet, not a mockup. The matching mountain, festival and foreground layers will be extended separately using the identical centered-two-thirds registration.

## Foreground

Reference/edit target: `assets/title-screen/layer-foreground.webp`.

> Use case: precise-object-edit, outpainting for a parallax game title screen. EDIT TARGET is the supplied transparent foreground layer. ZOOM OUT 1.5x: the entire existing source frame must now occupy exactly the CENTRAL TWO THIRDS of the output width AND height, with NEW scenery painted into the surrounding one-sixth margins at left/right/top/bottom. This is a camera pull-back, NOT filling transparent holes inside the existing composition. Output one 16:9 foreground layer with TRUE ALPHA TRANSPARENCY in the open central window above the road. Preserve the exact identity, pose, mustache, face, wheels, red body and yellow canopy of the cute vehicle Zerble. All existing art must retain its relative arrangement after the uniform 2/3 reduction: Zerble stays at about 60% across and 69% down of the final image. Continue the left and right trees into the new upper and side margins, extend fence rails, flowering bushes and grass out to both new edges, and continue the dirt road toward the new bottom edge with matching perspective and texture. New foliage should frame rather than obstruct the open transparent middle. Keep the original saturated warm pixel-painted retro illustration style. No mountains, sunset, tents or gate behind the opening. No new vehicles or characters. No text, logos, UI or bubbles. Return a single isolated foreground asset. Keep transparency truly transparent, never painted checkerboard. All four eventual parallax assets use the same centered-two-thirds registration.

## Mountains

Reference/edit target: `assets/title-screen/layer-mountains.webp`.

> Use case: precise-object-edit, outpainting. Create an extended individual mountain/lake parallax layer from the supplied image. ZOOM OUT 1.5x, shrinking ALL existing mountain/lake/forest features uniformly to two-thirds of their current width and height and placing that reduced original composition in the exact centered two-thirds rectangle of a 16:9 frame. Paint matching additional purple-blue mountain ridges and dark evergreen forest into the new left and right margins; continue forest and near-ground terrain down into the new bottom margin. The mountain skyline must remain an alpha-cutout with TRUE TRANSPARENCY above it, because a separate sunset layer goes behind. Extend natural hills, mountains and woods to the outer left/right edges; no vignette fading to empty margins. Preserve the warm orange-pink rim light, purple lake reflection and painted pixel-art style. Single isolated mountain landscape layer only. No sky fill, clouds or sun, no festival, no gates, no vehicles, no people, no bubbles, no labels or logos. Return genuine transparency, never a checkerboard or white backdrop. This layer shares exact centered-two-thirds source registration with three other layers.

## Festival

Reference/edit target: `assets/title-screen/layer-festival.webp`.

> Use case: precise-object-edit, outpainting. Edit the supplied transparent festival/gate layer into an extended individual parallax asset. Camera pull-back: ZOOM OUT 1.5x, reducing the whole supplied composition uniformly so the original source frame occupies exactly the CENTERED TWO THIRDS of the output width AND height. Outpaint more festival tents, low wooden fences and receding strings of warm glowing bulbs and small colorful pennant flags in the new left/right margins. Continue low festival ground/fencing downward into the new bottom margin as needed. Keep the central wooden entrance gate's exact structure, cloth canopy, proportions, position relative to the festival, decorations and warm lighting intact. Keep existing individual tents, musicians/stage and crowd silhouettes in the same relative arrangement. The upper sky area and openings between the bulbs, gate posts, fences and tents must remain genuinely transparent for separately animated sky/mountain layers. No sky/mountains/landscape backdrop, no vehicle, no tree foreground, no large new gate, no new close-up characters, no lettering, no bubbles, no UI. Match the saturated warm pixel-painted game-art texture. One isolated 16:9 transparent layer only. Do not render a checkerboard or grey/white background. All four parallax assets use the same exact central-two-thirds registration.

## Foreground alpha correction

Reference/edit target: the first foreground generation, `exec-541d7896-a3e7-4dce-9c5b-d94d0faa9953.png`.

> Use case: background-extraction / alpha cleanup. Edit target is this already extended game foreground image. Change ONLY the unwanted whitish/gray hazy background inside the opening between the trees, above the road and behind the vehicle: remove ALL of that background haze and replace it with fully transparent alpha=0. The whole sky window must be genuinely empty, including the upper center where the leaves end, the gaps around tree branches, and the space immediately behind and around the vehicle. There must be no faint white/gray matte, no opaque back panel and no checkerboard pixels. Preserve every visible tree leaf, trunk, fence, flower, bush, road pixel and the vehicle exactly where they are, with crisp natural transparent cutout edges. Do not change composition, framing, scale or add/remove any actual foreground objects. Return a single RGBA transparent foreground layer.
